# Part I. General Foundations
### Introduction to Philosophy of Complex Systems: A
#### A: Towards A Framework for Complex Systems

The first essay sets out to accomplish
1. Introduce and broadly review the domain of complex systems, considering the historical context.
2. The key systems properties at issue. 
3. A collection of sub-domains that are regrettably left out from the book.

The ultimate goal is to develop mature foundations for philosophy of complex systems.

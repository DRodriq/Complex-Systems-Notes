# Classical Mechanics — Concept Index

Classical mechanics done via variational principles (Lagrangian and
Hamiltonian formulations) is the physics track that installs phase-space
thinking, symmetries↔conservation laws (Noether), and the geometric viewpoint
on dynamics. Together with statistical mechanics and nonlinear dynamics, it
forms one of the three core physics pillars relevant to complexity science.

# Lagrangian Mechanics

## Orientation

Reformulation of classical mechanics based on the principle of least action.
The Lagrangian L = T − V (kinetic minus potential energy) is a scalar function
on configuration space; the dynamics follows from requiring that the action
∫L dt be stationary under variations of the trajectory, yielding the
Euler-Lagrange equations. Equivalent to Newton's laws for standard systems
but generalizes cleanly to constrained systems, field theories, and systems
on curved configuration manifolds.

## Connections

The variational principle is the conceptual seed. Noether's theorem
([[noether-theorem]]) connects continuous symmetries of the Lagrangian to
conserved quantities. Legendre transform of the Lagrangian yields the
Hamiltonian ([[hamiltonian-mechanics]]).

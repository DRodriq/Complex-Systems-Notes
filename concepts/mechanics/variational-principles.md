# Variational Principles

## Orientation

Family of principles that determine the trajectory, configuration, or
distribution of a physical system by requiring that some functional (action,
energy, free energy, entropy) be stationary under variations. Classical
mechanics uses the principle of least action; thermodynamic equilibrium is
a free-energy extremum; ML inference often reduces to variational bounds on
a free-energy-like functional.

## Connections

Unifies classical mechanics ([[lagrangian-mechanics]]), equilibrium statistical
mechanics (free-energy extremization), and variational inference in ML. One
of the most general organizing principles in theoretical science.

# Noether's Theorem

## Orientation

For every continuous symmetry of the action of a physical system, there is a
corresponding conserved quantity. Translation symmetry → momentum conservation;
rotational symmetry → angular momentum conservation; time-translation
symmetry → energy conservation. One of the deepest and most generative
results in theoretical physics: ties the structure of physical law to the
algebra of its symmetries.

## Connections

Symmetry↔conservation is a thinking tool well beyond physics: invariances of
loss landscapes in ML (parameter symmetries → conserved quantities along
gradient flow); graph automorphisms and invariant statistics on networks;
detailed-balance symmetries in equilibrium statistical mechanics.

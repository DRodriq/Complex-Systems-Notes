# Hamiltonian Mechanics

## Orientation

Reformulation of classical mechanics on phase space rather than configuration
space. Obtained from the Lagrangian via the Legendre transform. The
Hamiltonian H is typically the total energy expressed in canonical
coordinates (q, p); Hamilton's equations are a first-order symplectic flow.
Phase space has a natural symplectic structure, and many qualitative
properties of classical dynamics (conservation of phase-space volume
(Liouville), canonical transformations, Poisson brackets) are most naturally
stated in this language.

# Statistical Physics of Learning

## Orientation

A framing that treats machine learning — training, generalization, inference —
through the lens of statistical mechanics. Training losses become energies;
Bayesian posteriors become Boltzmann distributions; variational inference
becomes free-energy minimization; representation learning in deep networks
has been argued to share structure with the renormalization group. The frame
recovers intuitions from complex-systems physics (phase transitions,
universality, replica method) for understanding learning dynamics and
generalization.

# Phase Transitions

## Orientation

Qualitative macroscopic change in a system as a microscopic parameter
(temperature, density, coupling strength, etc.) crosses a critical value.
First-order transitions involve discontinuous change in an order parameter;
second-order (continuous) transitions involve divergent susceptibility and
correlation length. Near continuous transitions, systems exhibit scale
invariance and universal critical exponents.

## Connections

Phase transitions appear beyond physics wherever a system exhibits collective
reorganization at a control parameter: percolation thresholds in random graphs,
jamming transitions in granular and optimization problems, epidemic outbreak
thresholds, the edge of chaos in [[edge-of-chaos]], learning phase transitions
in neural network training, synchronization transitions in Kuramoto systems.

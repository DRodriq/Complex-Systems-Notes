# Statistical Mechanics — Concept Index

Statistical mechanics is the physics of ensembles: how macroscopic behavior
emerges from probability distributions over microscopic states. It is one of
the two principal physical foundations of complexity science (the other being
nonlinear dynamics). Its central contributions — entropy as a counting
argument, phase transitions, universality, and the renormalization group — are
among the most powerful thinking tools developed in 20th-century science.

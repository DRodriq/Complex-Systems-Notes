# Statistical Ensemble

## Orientation

A probability distribution over microstates of a system, chosen to match a
given set of macroscopic constraints. Microcanonical ensemble: fixed energy.
Canonical ensemble: fixed temperature (energy exchanged with a reservoir).
Grand canonical ensemble: fixed temperature and chemical potential (energy
and particles exchanged). The ensemble is the bookkeeping frame — different
constraints produce different partition functions and different natural
thermodynamic variables.

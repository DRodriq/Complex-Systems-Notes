# Renormalization Group

## Orientation

A systematic procedure for understanding how the effective description of a
physical system changes under coarse-graining — integrating out short-distance
(high-frequency) degrees of freedom and asking what equations govern what
remains. The procedure defines a flow in the space of coupling constants.
Fixed points of that flow correspond to scale-invariant theories; the
universality classes of critical phenomena are exactly the basins of
attraction of those fixed points. The RG is both a technical method and one
of the most powerful thinking tools in physics: it says that what survives
coarse-graining is what matters, and identifies precisely what that is.

## Connections

Beyond physics, RG-like thinking appears wherever scale-dependent phenomena
matter: multi-scale structure in IE / clustering work, deep-learning
representation hierarchies (Mehta et al. frame deep learning as RG),
hierarchical community structure in networks, coarse-graining in agent-based
models. The technical RG generalizes to Monte Carlo RG and to Wilsonian
exact-RG formulations.

# Statistical Entropy

## Orientation

Entropy as defined in statistical mechanics: the logarithm of the number of
microstates compatible with a macroscopic constraint (Boltzmann), or more
generally the expected log-probability over a distribution of microstates
(Gibbs). Operates as a measure of multiplicity, disorder, or uncertainty
depending on framing.

## Connections

Mathematically identical in form to [[shannon-entropy]] (up to a multiplicative
constant). The physics vs. information-theory interpretations differ in what
the distribution is *over* and in the physical role of the logarithm base. Both
flavors unify under the max-entropy principle (Jaynes).

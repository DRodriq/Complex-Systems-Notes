# Applied Category Theory

> The research programme that brings categorical machinery — composition, functoriality, universal constructions — to bear on concrete scientific and engineering problems: dynamical systems, databases, control, learning, games, networks, language. Often abbreviated **ACT**.

## Orientation

Applied category theory took shape in the 2010s as an organized programme aimed at making category theory useful outside pure mathematics, with a particular emphasis on compositional modelling: representing a complex system as the composite of simpler ones along an explicit interface, in a way that respects categorical laws. The canonical introductory text is Fong & Spivak's *Seven Sketches in Compositionality* (2018); the canonical venue is the Applied Category Theory conference (ACT) and its associated **Adjoint School**, an annual mentored research programme.

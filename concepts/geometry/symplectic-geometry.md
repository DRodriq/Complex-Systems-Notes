# Symplectic Geometry

## Orientation

The geometry of manifolds equipped with a closed, non-degenerate 2-form (the
symplectic form). Phase space in Hamiltonian mechanics is canonically a
symplectic manifold; Hamilton's equations are the flow generated by the
Hamiltonian through the symplectic form. Conservation laws correspond to
symmetries (moment maps, Noether's theorem in its geometric form).

## Connections

Mathematical foundation of [[hamiltonian-mechanics]]. Distinct from (and
orthogonal to) Riemannian geometry — symplectic structure is about area,
not length. KAM theorem (on persistence of quasi-periodic motion under small
perturbations) lives here and ties to [[chaos-theory]].

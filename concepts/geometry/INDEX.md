# Geometry — Concept Index

Differential and Riemannian geometry give the mathematical language for
curved spaces, smooth structures, and intrinsic notions of distance, shape,
and curvature. They underlie general relativity, classical mechanics (Arnold's
symplectic formulation), information geometry, and the manifold perspective
on high-dimensional embeddings in ML.

*Vault editorial stance and scope decisions: [[synthesis/why-complexity]].*

## Concepts in this domain

- [[differential-geometry]] — smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, vector fields,
  differential forms
- [[riemannian-geometry]] — manifolds with a metric; geodesics, connections,
  curvature
- [[information-geometry]] — probability distributions as points on a
  Riemannian manifold with the Fisher metric
- [[symplectic-geometry]] — the geometry underlying Hamiltonian mechanics
- [[discrete-exterior-calculus]] — discrete reformulation of the calculus of
  differential forms on simplicial complexes; substrate of the Decapodes
  multiphysics work (cross-link to [[concepts/category-theory/INDEX]])

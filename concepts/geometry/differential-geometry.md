# Differential Geometry

## Orientation

The study of smooth manifolds — spaces that look locally like Euclidean space
but globally may curve, twist, or connect nontrivially — and the calculus
that can be done on them: tangent and cotangent spaces, vector fields,
differential forms, Lie derivatives. Provides the mathematical language for
curved configuration spaces, phase spaces, state manifolds, and (in ML) the
manifold hypothesis for data embedded in high-dimensional ambient spaces.

# Information Geometry

## Orientation

The study of families of probability distributions as Riemannian manifolds,
with the Fisher information matrix as the natural metric. Introduces dual
affine connections (e-connection and m-connection) and a family of divergence
functions of which KL divergence is one. Gradient descent on such a manifold
becomes natural-gradient descent; maximum-likelihood estimation becomes
geodesic projection.

Synthesizes [[shannon-entropy]], [[kl-divergence]], [[riemannian-geometry]],

# Riemannian Geometry

## Orientation

Differential geometry equipped with a metric — a smoothly varying inner
product on each tangent space — from which lengths, angles, volumes,
geodesics, and curvature (sectional, Ricci, scalar) are derived. The
mathematical home of general relativity and of intrinsic geometry: all the
geometric structure one can measure from inside the manifold, without a
privileged embedding.

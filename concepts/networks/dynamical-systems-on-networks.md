# Dynamical Systems on Networks

## Orientation

Dynamical processes flowing over a fixed network topology — the topology is
the substrate, the node states are the evolving variables. Canonical
examples: Kuramoto oscillators on a graph (synchronization), SIS/SIR models
(epidemic spreading), voter/opinion models, spin models on graphs. Topology
structure (degree distribution, clustering, community structure, modularity)
shapes dynamical outcomes in systematic ways that are absent from well-mixed
mean-field treatments.

## Connections

Natural bridge from [[chaos-theory]] / Strogatz-style dynamics into networks —
a one-step generalization of phase-space dynamics onto a graph substrate.
Partner to [[temporal-networks]] and [[adaptive-networks]]; each fixes a
different element of the topology-state-timing triad.

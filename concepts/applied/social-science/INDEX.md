# Applied Social Science Domain

> Complexity science approaches to human social systems: cultural evolution, historical dynamics, cooperation, hunter-gatherer societies, and ABM of social phenomena.

## What This Domain Covers

Social science applications of complexity science include both evolutionary/adaptationist accounts of human behavior (evolutionary psychology, cultural evolution, behavioral ecology) and formal complexity models of social dynamics (Axelrod's cooperation models, Turchin's cliodynamics, ABM of social systems).

The vault's existing material skews toward evolutionary anthropology and hunter-gatherer studies (Hadza, Marlowe, Kelly), cooperation theory (Axelrod), and historical dynamics (Turchin). These reflect the vault author's interests more than a systematic coverage of the field.

## Anthropological and Political ABM

A coherent sub-thread on agent-based modeling of human social systems, particularly in archaeology and historical anthropology.

**Key sources**:
- Cail, R. & Dyke, J. (2019). ForagerNetABM — modeling hunter-gatherer mobility and interaction.
- Lake, M. (2014). "Trends in Archaeological Simulation." *Journal of Archaeological Method and Theory*.
- Premo, L. S. (2006). "Agent-based models as behavioral laboratories for evolutionary anthropology." *American Journal of Physical Anthropology*.
- Kohler, T. & Gumerman, G. (eds.) (2000). *Dynamics in Human and Primate Societies: Agent-Based Modeling of Social and Spatial Processes*.
- [[sources/papers/perreault-cultural-transmission-foragers-2011]] — Perreault & Brantingham on mobility and cultural transmission.

**Common ABM frameworks** (specifying landscape, agents, resource dynamics, social rules, scaling):
- **Archaeological Simulation Protocols** (Lake, 2014) — workflow for rigor, transparency, replication
- **OpenABM / CoMSES Net** — Community of Modeling for Social-Ecological Systems; repository + framework hub
- **Paleoscape Models** — GIS + ABM hybrids modeling resource distribution, mobility, demography in African archaeology
- **DISPERSCA** — cellular-automata ABM for Iberian hunter-gatherer mobility
- See also: NetLogo, Repast, MASON, Mesa as base platforms

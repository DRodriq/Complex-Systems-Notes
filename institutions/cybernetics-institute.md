# Cybernetics Institute (UK)

> UK-based independent research organization focused on categorical cybernetics, applied category theory, and the renewal of the cybernetics research programme. Hosts seminars, publishes a public blog, and acts as a coordination point for the categorical-cybernetics community around Hedges and collaborators at the University of Strathclyde.

## Focus and Role

The Cybernetics Institute frames its work as a continuation of the 20th-century cybernetics project — control, feedback, goal-directedness, the science of "circular causation" — but with the categorical machinery (lenses, optics, `Para(Optic)`, polynomial functors) that the original cybernetics movement lacked. Particularly active in compositional game theory, categorical accounts of learning, and active-inference / Bayesian-brain formalisms.

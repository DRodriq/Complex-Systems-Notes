# Applied Complexity Network (ACtioN)

> SFI's external network of businesses, governments, and organizations that engage with complexity science research and its practical applications.

## Focus and Role
ACtioN (Applied Complexity Network) is the Santa Fe Institute's outreach and engagement program for non-academic organizations — businesses, NGOs, and government agencies — interested in applying complexity science insights to real-world problems. Members receive access to SFI research, seminars, and workshops, and the program channels applied questions back to SFI researchers.

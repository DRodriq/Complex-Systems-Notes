# New England Complex Systems Institute (NECSI)

> An independent research and education institute in Cambridge, MA, founded by Yaneer Bar-Yam as an alternative center for complexity science outside SFI.

## Focus and Role
NECSI conducts research and education in complex systems, with a focus on multiscale analysis and applications to policy, healthcare, economics, and social dynamics. Bar-Yam's framework of complexity mismatch — the idea that management hierarchies fail when their complexity is less than the systems they manage — is NECSI's central practical contribution.

# University of Washington NeuroAI Lab

> The NeuroAI Lab at the University of Washington, led by Eli Shlizerman, studying neural dynamics and neuroscience-inspired artificial intelligence.

## Focus and Role
The UW NeuroAI Lab conducts research at the intersection of applied mathematics, neuroscience, and machine learning. Key research threads include neural circuit modeling as dynamical systems, data-driven discovery of neural dynamics, and neuroscience-inspired AI architectures. The lab connects dynamical systems theory to modern neural network research.

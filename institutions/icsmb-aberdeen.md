# Institute for Complex Systems and Mathematical Biology (ICSMB)

> A research institute at the University of Aberdeen applying complex systems theory and mathematical biology to life sciences questions.

## Focus and Role
ICSMB brings together mathematicians, physicists, and biologists at Aberdeen to study biological complexity using mathematical and computational tools. Research spans gene regulatory networks, ecological dynamics, and developmental biology from a systems perspective.

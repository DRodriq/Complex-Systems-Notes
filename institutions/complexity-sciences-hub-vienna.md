# Complexity Science Hub Vienna

> A European research hub for complexity science based in Vienna, Austria, applying complex systems methods to social, economic, and health systems.

## Focus and Role
The Complexity Science Hub Vienna conducts research applying network science, statistical physics, and agent-based modeling to large-scale social phenomena — economic dynamics, pandemics, urban systems, and social inequality. It has one of the largest complexity science research groups in Europe and maintains major datasets on social and economic dynamics.

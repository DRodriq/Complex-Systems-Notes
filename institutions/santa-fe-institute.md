# Santa Fe Institute

> The world's leading research center for complexity science, founded in 1984 in Santa Fe, New Mexico, dedicated to the study of complex adaptive systems across disciplines.

## Focus and Role
SFI operates as a small, independent research institute with no graduate students and a rotating cast of postdocs, resident faculty, and visiting researchers. Its model — bringing together scientists from physics, economics, biology, computer science, and other fields to work on shared questions about complexity — has been enormously influential. SFI invented or catalyzed the fields of complexity economics, artificial life, and agent-based social science.

# Georgia Tech Research Institute (GTRI)

> Applied-research arm of the Georgia Institute of Technology, in Atlanta. Conducts contracted R&D across defense, aerospace, electronics, and information sciences. Notable for the vault as the affiliation of Jesús Arias and Maia Gatlin, co-authors on the 2024 Decapodes paper bringing AlgebraicJulia / categorical compositional modelling to multiphysics PDE simulation.

## Focus and Role

GTRI sits institutionally between Georgia Tech academic research and the applied / contracted research world. Its involvement in the Decapodes work indicates GTRI as one of the practical user nodes for AlgebraicJulia outside the core academic / Topos community.

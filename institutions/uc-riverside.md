# University of California, Riverside (Baez Network-Theory Programme)

> UC Riverside is included in the vault primarily as the institutional home of John Baez's long-running **Network Theory programme** (~2010–2020+), which produced the categorical foundations for treating models as open systems: **decorated cospans**, **structured cospans**, **open Petri nets**, and the functorial-semantics treatment of reaction networks and Markov processes.

## Focus and Role

Baez's network-theory programme operated through a public-facing blog series ("Network Theory" on the *n*-Category Café), a series of papers, and a cluster of PhD students who carried the work forward. The output became the categorical substrate for applied compositional modelling — picked up at Topos Institute, AlgebraicJulia, and the broader ACT community.

Origin point for [[concepts/category-theory/open-systems]] and a large fraction of the [[concepts/category-theory/functorial-semantics]] sources. The vault's category-theory cluster owes much of its open-systems substrate to work that came out of this programme.

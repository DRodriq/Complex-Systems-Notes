# Potsdam Complexity Science Group

> A research group at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK) applying nonlinear dynamics and network science to climate and social systems.

## Focus and Role
The Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research hosts a major complexity science research program, associated with Jürgen Kurths, that applies synchronization theory, network methods, and nonlinear time series analysis to climate dynamics, power grid stability, and social-ecological systems. PIK is one of Europe's leading centers for climate complexity research.

# Topos Institute

> Independent research institute in Berkeley, California, focused on applied category theory. Central hub for the ACT community; hosts a public seminar series, the Topos Colloquium, and contributes to open-source compositional-modelling tooling (Catlab.jl, the AlgebraicJulia ecosystem).

## Focus and Role

Topos was founded in 2020 to give applied category theory institutional stability outside the standard university structure. The institute pursues research and tool-building in roughly equal measure, with particular emphasis on compositional modelling, scientific computing, and the categorical foundations of systems theory. Its small staff includes several of the most active figures in ACT, and its open seminar series functions as one of the field's principal forums.

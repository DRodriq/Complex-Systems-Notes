# Center for Complex Adaptive Agent Systems Simulation (CAS2)

> A simulation center at Argonne National Laboratory focused on agent-based modeling of complex adaptive systems in energy, infrastructure, and social domains.

## Focus and Role
CAS2 at Argonne develops and applies large-scale agent-based models to complex systems in energy infrastructure, social systems, and national security. The center is associated with Charles Macal's work on ABM methodology and has contributed to methodological standards in agent-based social simulation.

# Binghamton Center of Complex Systems (CoCo)

> A research center at Binghamton University (SUNY) focused on complex systems, evolution, and collective behavior.

## Focus and Role
The Center for Collective Dynamics of Complex Systems (CoCo) at Binghamton University, led by Hiroki Sayama, conducts research on evolutionary dynamics, swarm intelligence, network evolution, and complexity science education. It has contributed to open educational resources in complexity science and to empirical research on collective behavior.

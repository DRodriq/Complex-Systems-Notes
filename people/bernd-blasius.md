# Bernd Blasius

> German theoretical ecologist at the University of Oldenburg, working on nonlinear dynamics of ecological systems and adaptive networks.

## Role and Contributions
Co-author (with Thilo Gross) of the 2008 adaptive-networks review. Broader program spans ecosystem dynamics, synchronization, invasion dynamics, and the interplay of ecological dynamics with the networks that constrain them.

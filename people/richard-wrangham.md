# Richard W. Wrangham

> British primatologist at Harvard known for studying chimpanzee violence, the cooking hypothesis of human evolution, and human aggression.

## Role and Contributions
Wrangham studies primate social behavior, particularly chimpanzee lethal aggression and its implications for human violence. His "cooking hypothesis" argues that the invention of cooking was the key driver of human anatomical and cognitive evolution. He also developed the "demonic males" hypothesis about the evolutionary roots of male violence in apes and humans.

## Key Works
- Demonic Males (with Peterson, 1996)
- Catching Fire: How Cooking Made Us Human (2009)

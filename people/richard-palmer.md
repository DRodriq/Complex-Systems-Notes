# Richard Palmer

> Physicist at Duke University who contributed to spin glass theory and co-organized SFI economics workshops on complexity.

## Role and Contributions
Palmer worked on spin glass models — disordered magnetic systems with many competing interactions — that became an important metaphor for complex adaptive systems and fitness landscapes. He contributed to the SFI economics program, bringing spin glass concepts to economic modeling.

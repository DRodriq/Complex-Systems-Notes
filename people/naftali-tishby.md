# Naftali Tishby

> Israeli computer scientist and physicist at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem (d. 2021), originator of the Information Bottleneck framework and a central figure in the information-theoretic analysis of deep learning.

## Role and Contributions
Tishby (with Fernando Pereira and William Bialek) introduced the Information Bottleneck method in 1999 — a principle for extracting relevant information under compression. His later work argued that deep neural networks, viewed through IB, undergo distinct "fitting" and "compression" phases during training, connecting IB to generalization.

Bridges [[shannon-entropy]], [[mutual-information]], [[neural-networks]], and [[stat-physics-of-learning]]. Information Bottleneck is one of the few genuinely information-theoretic lenses on deep learning.

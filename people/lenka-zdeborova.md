# Lenka Zdeborová

> Czech-French statistical physicist at EPFL (Statistical Physics of Computation Laboratory), one of the leading figures in the statistical-mechanics-of-ML community.

## Role and Contributions
Zdeborová's work applies spin-glass methods and the replica/cavity techniques from statistical physics to inference, compressed sensing, community detection on graphs, and the theoretical analysis of deep networks. She helped establish that phase transitions in inference problems have direct algorithmic consequences.

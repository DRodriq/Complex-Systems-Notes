# David Schwab

> American statistical physicist at the CUNY Graduate Center (ITS / Initiative for the Theoretical Sciences), working on connections between the renormalization group and representation learning in deep networks.

## Role and Contributions
Schwab (with Pankaj Mehta) advanced the argument that deep neural networks perform a coarse-graining analogous to the renormalization group. Co-author of the 2019 Mehta et al. review, and of foundational papers on RG–deep-learning correspondences.

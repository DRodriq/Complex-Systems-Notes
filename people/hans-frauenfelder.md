# Hans Frauenfelder

> Austrian-American physicist (1922–2022) who pioneered the study of protein dynamics, introducing the concept of conformational substates and energy landscapes in biomolecules.

## Role and Contributions
Frauenfelder discovered that proteins exist in many nearly-degenerate conformational substates with complex, hierarchical energy landscape structure. His work connected the physics of disordered systems (like spin glasses) to biological function, showing that protein dynamics is governed by a rugged energy landscape — a concept central to both biophysics and complexity science.

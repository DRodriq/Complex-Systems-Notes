# Klaus Puettmann

> Forest ecologist at Oregon State University who applies complex systems thinking to silviculture and forest management.

## Role and Contributions
Puettmann advocates for managing forests as complex adaptive systems, co-authoring the influential book on CAS approaches to silviculture with Christian Messier. He argues for management approaches that embrace uncertainty, promote structural diversity, and build adaptive capacity in forest ecosystems.

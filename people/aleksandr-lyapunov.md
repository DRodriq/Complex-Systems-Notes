# Aleksandr Lyapunov

> Russian mathematician (1857–1918) who developed the rigorous theory of stability in dynamical systems, introducing Lyapunov functions and Lyapunov exponents.

## Role and Contributions
Lyapunov's 1892 doctoral dissertation "The General Problem of the Stability of Motion" established the mathematical foundations for analyzing whether small perturbations grow or decay in dynamical systems. His methods — Lyapunov functions (energy-like quantities) and linearization — remain the primary tools for stability analysis. Lyapunov exponents, which quantify the rate of divergence of nearby trajectories, are the primary measure of chaos.

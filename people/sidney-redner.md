# Sidney Redner

> Statistical physicist at the Santa Fe Institute and emeritus at Boston University, known for work on random walks, first-passage processes, aggregation, and stat-physics methods applied to networks.

## Role and Contributions
Redner's *A Guide to First-Passage Processes* (2001) is the canonical treatment of first-passage problems. His later work applied statistical mechanics tooling to network science, citation dynamics, and social systems. SFI external/resident across his career.

## Connections
Exemplar of the stat-physicist-turned-complex-systems archetype. Connects [[statistical-mechanics]], random walks / diffusion, and [[networks]].

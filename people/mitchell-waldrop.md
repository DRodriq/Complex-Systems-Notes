# Mitchell M. Waldrop

> Science journalist who wrote Complexity (1992), the defining popular account of the founding of the Santa Fe Institute and the emergence of complexity science.

## Role and Contributions
Waldrop, a science writer for Science magazine, embedded himself in the SFI community and wrote the best-selling narrative account of how SFI was founded and how complexity science emerged. His book introduced complexity science to a broad public audience and remains the most cited popular account of the field's history.

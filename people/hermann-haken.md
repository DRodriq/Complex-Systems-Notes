# Hermann Haken

> German physicist (1927–) who founded synergetics as a theory of self-organization, developing the order parameter and slaving principle framework.

## Role and Contributions
Haken, based at the University of Stuttgart, developed synergetics as a general theory of self-organization in physical and biological systems. Starting from laser physics — which he explained as a cooperative phenomenon — he generalized the theory to any system undergoing a phase transition to ordered behavior. The slaving principle (fast modes enslaved to slow order parameters) provides a systematic way to reduce high-dimensional systems near bifurcations.

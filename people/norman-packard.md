# Norman Packard

> Physicist and complexity scientist who contributed to the early chaos revolution at Santa Cruz, co-founded Prediction Company, and developed the concept of the edge of chaos independently of Langton.

## Role and Contributions
Packard was part of the "Dynamical Systems Collective" at UC Santa Cruz with Farmer, Shaw, and Crutchfield, making early contributions to chaos theory, attractor reconstruction, and time series analysis. He independently proposed that complex computation and adaptability occur near the edge of chaos. He co-founded Prediction Company with Doyne Farmer to apply these ideas to financial trading.

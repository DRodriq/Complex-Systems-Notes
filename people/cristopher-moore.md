# Cristopher Moore

> Theoretical computer scientist and physicist at the Santa Fe Institute, working at the intersection of computational complexity, statistical physics, and network science.

## Role and Contributions
Moore has done foundational work on phase transitions in computational problems (SAT, coloring), spectral methods for community detection in networks, and stochastic block models. His book *The Nature of Computation* (with Stephan Mertens) is a rare treatment that braids computational complexity with statistical physics perspectives.

## Connections
SFI resident faculty. Bridges the [[computational-complexity]] and [[statistical-mechanics]] domains; touches [[community-structure]] via spectral methods.

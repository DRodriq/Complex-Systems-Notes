# John Holland

> Computer scientist and psychologist (1929–2015) who invented genetic algorithms, developed the theory of complex adaptive systems, and was a founding figure at SFI.

## Role and Contributions
Holland invented genetic algorithms in the 1960s–70s as a general method for machine learning and search inspired by biological evolution. He developed the theoretical framework of complex adaptive systems (CAS), including classifier systems (rule-based adaptive agents), schema theory, and the concept of emergence through building blocks and tags. He was one of SFI's founding external faculty and its intellectual linchpin for decades.

# Stefan Thurner

> Austrian complexity scientist and medical doctor at the Medical University of Vienna who studies complex systems from an information-theoretic and network perspective, with applications to health and society.

## Role and Contributions
Thurner leads the Complexity Science Hub Vienna and has developed theoretical extensions to statistical mechanics for complex systems (with Hanel and Klimek). His empirical work applies network and complexity methods to healthcare, economics, and social systems. He is unusual in complexity science for combining rigorous mathematical theory with large-scale empirical health data analysis.

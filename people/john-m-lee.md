# John M. Lee

> American mathematician, professor emeritus at the University of Washington, known for a trilogy of graduate textbooks on differential geometry.

## Role and Contributions
Author of *Introduction to Topological Manifolds*, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds* (2nd ed 2012), and *Riemannian Manifolds*. The smooth-manifolds text is a standard modern entry to differential geometry at the graduate level.

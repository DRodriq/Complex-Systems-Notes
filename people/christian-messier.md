# Christian Messier

> Forest ecologist who applies complex adaptive systems theory to silviculture and forest management.

## Role and Contributions
Messier is a professor at UQAM and Université de Montréal whose work advocates for managing forests as complex adaptive systems. Co-authoring with Klaus Puettmann, he argues that traditional forestry's command-and-control approach must be replaced with adaptive management that embraces uncertainty, promotes diversity, and builds resilience.

# Claude Shannon

> American mathematician and electrical engineer (1916–2001) who founded information theory, establishing the mathematical basis for communication, data compression, and cryptography.

## Role and Contributions
Shannon's 1948 paper "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" created information theory from scratch, introducing entropy as a measure of information, the bit as the fundamental unit, and the channel capacity theorem showing the maximum rate at which information can be transmitted over a noisy channel. Shannon entropy is now central to complexity science as a measure of uncertainty and as a baseline against which more refined complexity measures are defined.

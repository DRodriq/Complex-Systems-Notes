# Seth Lloyd

> MIT physicist and quantum computing pioneer who has contributed to the thermodynamics of computation, quantum information theory, and measures of complexity.

## Role and Contributions
Lloyd made foundational contributions to quantum computing — proving that quantum computers can simulate quantum systems efficiently — and to the thermodynamics of computation. With Murray Gell-Mann, he developed the framework of total information and effective complexity. He has also written on the universe-as-quantum-computer metaphor, arguing that physical processes are fundamentally computational.

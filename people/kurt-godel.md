# Kurt Gödel

> Austrian-American logician (1906–1978) who proved the incompleteness theorems, establishing fundamental limits on what formal systems can prove about themselves.

## Role and Contributions
Gödel's 1931 incompleteness theorems proved that any sufficiently powerful consistent formal system contains true statements that cannot be proven within the system (first theorem), and cannot prove its own consistency (second theorem). These results are foundational for computer science (via Turing's undecidability), philosophy of mathematics, and have been invoked — sometimes controversially — in discussions of complexity and the limits of computation.

## Key Works
- On Formally Undecidable Propositions of Principia Mathematica (1931)

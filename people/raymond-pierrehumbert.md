# Raymond T. Pierrehumbert

> Geophysicist and climate scientist at the University of Oxford who works on planetary climates, climate dynamics, and exoplanet atmospheres.

## Role and Contributions
Pierrehumbert has contributed to the physical understanding of Earth's climate system, paleoclimate (particularly Snowball Earth), and the climates of exoplanets. His textbook "Principles of Planetary Climate" is the definitive physical treatment of climate science. He is also a public advocate for climate action.

# Manfred Eigen

> German biophysicist (1927–2019) and Nobel laureate who developed quasispecies theory and hypercycles as models for the origin of life and molecular evolution.

## Role and Contributions
Eigen received the 1967 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for fast chemical reactions work. His later work on molecular evolution was more influential for complexity science: quasispecies theory models the evolution of RNA populations as clouds of mutants around a fitness peak, with implications for viral evolution. The hypercycle model proposed how self-replicating molecules could cooperate to produce proto-cells.

## Key Works
- The Hypercycle (with Schuster, 1979)
- Quasispecies: From Theory to Experimental Systems

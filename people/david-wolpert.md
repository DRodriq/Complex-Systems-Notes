# David Wolpert

> Mathematician and physicist at SFI known for the No Free Lunch theorems, thermodynamics of computation, and theoretical work on collective intelligence.

## Role and Contributions
Wolpert proved the No Free Lunch theorems showing that no optimization algorithm outperforms random search on the full space of all possible problems — a foundational result in machine learning and optimization. At SFI, he has worked on the thermodynamics of computation (extending Landauer), collective intelligence (COINs), and mathematical foundations of complex systems.

## Key Works
- No Free Lunch theorems (with Macready)
- Thermodynamics of computation

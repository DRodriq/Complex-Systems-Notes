# Murray Gell-Mann

> Nobel-winning physicist (1929–2019) who discovered quarks, co-founded the Santa Fe Institute, and contributed the concept of effective complexity to complexity science.

## Role and Contributions
Gell-Mann received the 1969 Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering quarks and developing the quark model of hadrons. In the 1980s he became deeply interested in complexity science and was instrumental in founding SFI, providing both intellectual leadership and fundraising gravitas. His contributions to complexity science include the concept of effective complexity (as distinguished from algorithmic information or Shannon entropy) and the framework of complex adaptive systems.

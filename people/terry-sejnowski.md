# Terry Sejnowski

> American computational neuroscientist at the Salk Institute (Computational Neurobiology Laboratory) and UCSD, a founding figure in the field.

## Role and Contributions
Sejnowski (with Geoffrey Hinton) introduced the Boltzmann machine in the 1980s — one of the earliest stat-mech-inspired neural network models. His group's work spans learning algorithms, spiking network models, and the theoretical principles of neural coding. Co-founder of the NeurIPS conference.

## Connections
Seed figure connecting [[statistical-mechanics]], [[neural-networks]], and theoretical neuroscience. Boltzmann machines are an early instance of [[stat-physics-of-learning]].

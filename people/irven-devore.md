# Irven DeVore

> American anthropologist at Harvard (1934–2014) who pioneered the study of primate behavior and hunter-gatherer societies, particularly the !Kung San.

## Role and Contributions
DeVore was a founding figure in primatology and hunter-gatherer research, conducting early field studies of baboon social behavior and co-leading the Harvard Kalahari Research Group's studies of the !Kung San. His work established comparative frameworks for understanding human evolution through primate and hunter-gatherer data.

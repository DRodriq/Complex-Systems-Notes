# Kenneth Stanley

> Computer scientist and AI researcher known for inventing NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies) and for work on open-endedness in evolutionary computation.

## Role and Contributions
Stanley, now at OpenAI after positions at University of Central Florida, invented NEAT — the foundational algorithm for evolving neural network topology alongside weights. He later developed HyperNEAT (evolving large networks via geometric patterns) and novelty search (rewarding exploration rather than objective pursuit). His theoretical work on open-endedness explores how to build systems that continually generate novel complexity without stagnating.

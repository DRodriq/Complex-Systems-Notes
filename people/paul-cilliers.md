# Paul Cilliers

> South African philosopher (1956–2011) who developed a postmodern-influenced philosophy of complexity, arguing for the ethical and epistemological implications of complex systems thinking.

## Role and Contributions
Cilliers, based at Stellenbosch University, wrote "Complexity and Postmodernism" (1998), arguing that connectionist models of complex systems instantiate postmodern ideas about distributed meaning and context-dependence. He developed an ethical framework for decision-making under complexity, emphasizing modesty, responsibility, and the recognition of irreducible uncertainty. His work is influential in management science and complexity-informed policy.

# Noah Goodman

> Cognitive scientist and AI researcher at Stanford who works on probabilistic programming, Bayesian models of cognition, and language model reasoning.

## Role and Contributions
Goodman develops probabilistic programming languages (WebPPL, Church) as tools for building Bayesian models of cognition and language. His work on pragmatic language use (the Rational Speech Act framework) models communication as Bayesian reasoning. More recently he has worked on LLM-based scientific discovery and the relationship between language models and probabilistic generative models.

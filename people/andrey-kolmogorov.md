# Andrey Kolmogorov

> Soviet mathematician (1903–1987) of extraordinary breadth, contributing foundational work to probability theory, turbulence, algorithmic information theory, and classical mechanics.

## Role and Contributions
Kolmogorov axiomatized probability theory (1933), developed the theory of turbulent energy cascades (Kolmogorov scaling in turbulence), contributed to the KAM theorem on stability of Hamiltonian systems, and co-founded algorithmic information theory (Kolmogorov complexity). Each of these contributions is of major importance to different threads in complexity science.

## Key Works
- Foundations of the Theory of Probability (1933)
- Kolmogorov complexity (with Solomonoff, Chaitin)

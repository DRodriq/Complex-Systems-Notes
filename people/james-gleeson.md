# James Gleeson

> Irish applied mathematician at the University of Limerick (MACSI — Mathematics Applications Consortium for Science and Industry), working on dynamics on networks, cascade processes, and network epidemics.

## Role and Contributions
Gleeson developed analytical methods for approximate master equations and cascade dynamics on networks, giving tractable predictions for phenomena that would otherwise require full simulation. Co-author (with Mason Porter) of the widely used 2016 tutorial *Dynamical Systems on Networks*.

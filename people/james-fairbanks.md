# James Fairbanks

> Computer scientist at the University of Florida; co-leader of the AlgebraicJulia ecosystem with [[evan-patterson|Evan Patterson]]. Work bridges category-theoretic foundations and practical scientific computing — Catlab.jl, AlgebraicPetri.jl, AlgebraicDynamics.jl, and the Decapodes.jl multiphysics PDE library. Trains and collaborates with a cluster of researchers (Morris, Baas, Libkind, Halter) carrying compositional modelling into working code.

## Key Works

- [[sources/papers/patterson-categorical-data-structures-2022]] — ACSets/Catlab.jl.
- [[sources/papers/libkind-baas-patterson-fairbanks-operadic-dynamical-2021]] — operadic dynamical-systems modelling.
- [[sources/papers/libkind-structured-epidemic-2022]] — structured epidemic modelling, typed Petri nets, `typed_product` stratification.
- [[sources/papers/morris-decapodes-2024]] — Decapodes.jl: DEC + categorical composition for multiphysics PDEs.

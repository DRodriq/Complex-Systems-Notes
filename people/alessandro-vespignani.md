# Alessandro Vespignani

> Italian-American physicist at Northeastern University (Network Science Institute), trained in statistical physics, now a leading figure in network epidemiology and computational social science.

## Role and Contributions
Vespignani developed much of the theoretical framework for epidemic spreading on scale-free networks, showing that standard epidemic thresholds vanish in the thermodynamic limit for certain power-law topologies. His group's GLEAM model was prominent in COVID-19 forecasting. Co-author of *Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks* (2008).

## Connections
Prototype example of the stat-physicist → network-epidemiology migration. Touches [[scale-free-networks]], [[dynamical-systems-on-networks]], [[temporal-networks]], and epidemiology applications.

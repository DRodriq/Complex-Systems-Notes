# B. A. Huberman

> Physicist and computer scientist (Bernardo Huberman) who made early contributions to chaos in nonlinear systems and to the science of the web and social computation.

## Role and Contributions
Huberman worked at Xerox PARC and later HP Labs, making contributions to chaos theory, the dynamics of the World Wide Web, and social computation. He studied the dynamics of traffic on the web, the scaling of website visitation patterns, and the collective behavior of online communities. An early figure in applying physics methods to internet and social phenomena.

## Key Works
- The Laws of the Web (2001)

# Ellen Kuhl

> Mechanical engineer and biophysicist at Stanford who works on computational mechanics of living systems, including brain mechanics, cardiac dynamics, and growth.

## Role and Contributions
Kuhl develops continuum mechanics models of biological growth and remodeling, applying computational methods to understand how brains fold, hearts remodel after infarction, and tissues grow. Her work connects mechanics to biological complexity and medical applications.

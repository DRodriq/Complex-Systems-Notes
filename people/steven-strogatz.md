# Steven Strogatz

> Applied mathematician at Cornell University known for his textbook on nonlinear dynamics, the small-world network model, and contributions to synchronization theory.

## Role and Contributions
Strogatz wrote the definitive introductory textbook on nonlinear dynamics and chaos, celebrated for making the subject accessible without sacrificing rigor. With Duncan Watts, he introduced the Watts-Strogatz small-world network model (1998). He has also made significant contributions to synchronization theory, particularly the analysis of the Kuramoto model of coupled oscillators. His popular books (Sync, The Joy of X, Infinite Powers) have brought mathematics to broad audiences.

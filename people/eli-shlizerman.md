# Eli Shlizerman

> Applied mathematician and NeuroAI researcher at the University of Washington who studies neural dynamics, neural network theory, and neuroscience-inspired computation.

## Role and Contributions
Shlizerman directs the UW NeuroAI Lab and works at the intersection of applied mathematics, neuroscience, and machine learning. His research examines neural circuits as dynamical systems, develops mathematical models of biological neural computation, and explores how neuroscience principles can inspire new AI architectures.

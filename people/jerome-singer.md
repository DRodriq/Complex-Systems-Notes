# Jerome L. Singer

> American psychologist (1924–2021) at Yale known for his research on daydreaming, imagination, and the role of fantasy in mental life.

## Role and Contributions
Singer pioneered the empirical study of daydreaming and mind-wandering, establishing that inner life is a significant aspect of mental activity with developmental, motivational, and creative dimensions. Source note — peripheral to the vault's core complexity science focus.

## Key Works
- The Inner World of Daydreaming (1975)

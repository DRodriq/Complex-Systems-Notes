# Larry Abbott

> American theoretical neuroscientist at Columbia University (Zuckerman Institute / Center for Theoretical Neuroscience), trained as a theoretical physicist.

## Role and Contributions
Abbott (with Peter Dayan) co-authored *Theoretical Neuroscience* (2001), the standard graduate textbook in the field. His work spans dynamics of recurrent networks, synaptic plasticity rules, and the analysis of high-dimensional neural activity.

## Connections
Theoretical neuroscience archetype — physics training applied to neural dynamics. Relates to [[neural-networks]], dynamics in recurrent networks (sibling concept to [[dynamical-systems-on-networks]]).

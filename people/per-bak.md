# Per Bak

> Danish physicist (1947–2002) who proposed self-organized criticality (SOC) as a universal mechanism for the emergence of complex, scale-free behavior in nature.

## Role and Contributions
Bak, working at Brookhaven, introduced self-organized criticality in 1987 with Chao Tang and Kurt Wiesenfeld using the sandpile model. SOC proposes that many complex systems — earthquakes, forest fires, evolution, brain activity — naturally self-tune to a critical state, producing scale-free (power-law) statistics without external tuning. With Sneppen, he applied SOC to evolutionary dynamics.

# Wojciech H. Zurek

> Polish-American physicist at Los Alamos known for contributions to quantum decoherence, quantum Darwinism, and the thermodynamics of information.

## Role and Contributions
Zurek has worked on the quantum-to-classical transition via decoherence, proposing "quantum Darwinism" — the idea that classical reality emerges when quantum information about a system is redundantly copied into the environment. He has also contributed to the thermodynamics of information processing, connecting Maxwell's demon to quantum measurement.

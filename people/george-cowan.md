# George A. Cowan

> Nuclear chemist and science administrator who was the primary founder and first chairman of the Santa Fe Institute.

## Role and Contributions
Cowan spent his career at Los Alamos National Laboratory as a radiochemist and science administrator. Disillusioned with the narrowing of scientific inquiry at national laboratories, he was the driving force behind founding the Santa Fe Institute in 1984, recruiting Murray Gell-Mann and others to create a new kind of interdisciplinary research institution. He served as SFI's first chairman and primary fundraiser.

# Bruno Latour

> French sociologist and philosopher of science (1947–2022) at Sciences Po Paris, founding figure of Actor-Network Theory and one of the central figures in late-20th-century STS.

## Role and Contributions
Latour's *Science in Action* (1987), *We Have Never Been Modern* (1991), and *Reassembling the Social* (2005) reshaped how the social sciences think about the interplay of human and non-human actors, scientific practice, and networks of relation. Actor-Network Theory is network thinking in the humanistic sense — not the graph-theoretic sense — but the two have productive resonances.

## Connections
Adjacent rather than central to the technical complexity pathway. Network thinking as a humanistic methodology. Late-career work on climate and the earth as a political-ecological actor.

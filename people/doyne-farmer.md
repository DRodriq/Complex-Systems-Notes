# Doyne Farmer

> Physicist, complexity scientist, and entrepreneur who made early contributions to chaos theory and founded the application of complex systems methods to financial markets.

## Role and Contributions
Farmer was part of the chaos revolution at Los Alamos in the late 1970s and early 80s, working with Norman Packard, Robert Shaw, and James Crutchfield (the "Dynamical Systems Collective"). He later co-founded Prediction Company (applying chaos theory to financial trading) and has been a resident researcher at SFI working on computational economics, market ecology, and green energy transition modeling.

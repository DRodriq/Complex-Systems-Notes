# Isabelle Stengers

> Belgian philosopher of science at the Université Libre de Bruxelles, trained in chemistry; long-time collaborator of Ilya Prigogine and one of the principal interpreters of nonequilibrium thermodynamics as philosophy.

## Role and Contributions
Co-author (with Prigogine) of *Order Out of Chaos* (1984), which introduced dissipative-structure thinking to a humanistic audience. Her independent work — *Cosmopolitics*, *Thinking with Whitehead*, *In Catastrophic Times* — engages philosophy of science, ecological politics, and Whitehead's process philosophy. Distinguished from pop-science expositions by rigor and philosophical depth.

## Connections
Prototype figure connecting [[self-organization]], [[phase-transitions]], and nonequilibrium thermodynamics to contemporary philosophy of science.

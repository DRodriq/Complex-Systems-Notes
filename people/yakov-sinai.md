# Yakov Sinai

> Russian-American mathematician at Princeton University, also a student of Kolmogorov and a central figure of the Moscow mathematical school.

## Role and Contributions
Sinai's work founded much of rigorous ergodic theory (Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy, Sinai billiards) and established deep connections between dynamical systems and statistical mechanics. Awarded the Abel Prize (2014) for his contributions. His work is load-bearing for the rigorous foundations of the statistical-mechanics–dynamical-systems bridge.

## Connections
Ergodic theory as the mathematical backbone connecting [[chaos-theory]] to [[statistical-mechanics]]. KS entropy is a dynamical analogue of [[shannon-entropy]].

# Paul Humphreys

> Philosopher of science at the University of Virginia who works on emergence, causation, computational science, and the philosophical implications of numerical simulation.

## Role and Contributions
Humphreys has contributed to the philosophy of emergence (particularly "fusion" as a type of emergence), the epistemology of computer simulation, and causation in complex systems. He co-edited the emergence anthology with Mark Bedau, providing philosophical analysis of the emergence concept across sciences.

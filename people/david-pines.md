# David Pines

> Condensed matter physicist and co-founder of the Santa Fe Institute who brought concepts from emergent phenomena in physics to the study of complex systems.

## Role and Contributions
Pines, a major figure in condensed matter physics, was one of SFI's founding scientists. He helped bring the physics perspective — particularly ideas about emergence, collective behavior, and phase transitions — to the interdisciplinary complexity program. He co-edited the first SFI economics volume with Anderson and Arrow.

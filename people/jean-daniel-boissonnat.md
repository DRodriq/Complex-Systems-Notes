# Jean-Daniel Boissonnat

> French computer scientist at INRIA known for computational geometry, Delaunay triangulations, and topological data analysis.

## Role and Contributions
Boissonnat is a leader in computational geometry and topological data analysis (TDA), contributing algorithms for geometric reconstruction, Delaunay complexes, and persistent homology. His work on the CGAL library and geometric data structures is foundational for computational geometry.

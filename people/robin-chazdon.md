# Robin L. Chazdon

> Tropical ecologist at the University of Connecticut known for research on tropical forest regeneration, secondary forests, and restoration ecology.

## Role and Contributions
Chazdon is a leading researcher on tropical secondary forest dynamics — how forests regenerate after disturbance — and on the role of regenerating forests in biodiversity conservation and carbon sequestration. Source note — peripheral to the vault's core complexity science focus.

# W. Brian Arthur

> Irish-American economist at SFI who pioneered complexity economics, including the theories of increasing returns, path dependence, and technology as an evolving system.

## Role and Contributions
Arthur, the first permanent external faculty at SFI, challenged neoclassical economics by demonstrating that increasing returns and positive feedbacks produce path dependence, lock-in, and multiple equilibria. His model of QWERTY and VHS lock-in became canonical. He developed the broader framework of complexity economics, viewing the economy as an evolving complex adaptive system rather than a static optimization problem.

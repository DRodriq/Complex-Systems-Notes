# Edward Norton Lorenz

> Meteorologist and mathematician (1917–2008) who discovered deterministic chaos through his work on atmospheric convection, introducing the Lorenz attractor and the butterfly effect.

## Role and Contributions
Lorenz discovered chaos in 1963 while simulating a simplified model of atmospheric convection. His three-variable system (the Lorenz equations) produced a strange attractor — bounded, non-periodic, sensitive to initial conditions. This discovery, made partly by accident when he re-ran a simulation from a truncated printout, demonstrated that deterministic systems could be unpredictable. His "butterfly effect" metaphor became the defining image of chaos theory.

## Key Works
- "Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow" (1963)
- The Essence of Chaos (1993)

# Kenneth Arrow

> American economist (1921–2017), Nobel laureate, and one of the most important economic theorists of the 20th century, who participated in SFI's founding economics workshops.

## Role and Contributions
Arrow made foundational contributions to general equilibrium theory, social choice theory (Arrow's Impossibility Theorem), and the economics of information. His participation in the first SFI economics workshop (1987) represented the legitimation of complexity economics by the most distinguished mainstream economist. Arrow was open to challenging equilibrium economics, providing intellectual cover for the heterodox SFI program.

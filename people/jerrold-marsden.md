# Jerrold E. Marsden

> American mathematician (1942–2010); foundational figure in **geometric mechanics**, symplectic geometry, and the geometric-numerical-integration / discrete-mechanics programme. Long-time Caltech faculty; advisor to many central figures in computational geometry and applied geometry, including Hirani and Leok. Co-author of the 2005 DEC paper.

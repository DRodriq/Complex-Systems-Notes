# Charles H. Bennett

> IBM physicist who made foundational contributions to the physics of information, quantum cryptography, and the thermodynamics of computation.

## Role and Contributions
Bennett resolved Maxwell's Demon by showing that the erasure of information, not its acquisition, requires thermodynamic work (Landauer's principle). He co-invented quantum cryptography (BB84 protocol with Gilles Brassard) and contributed to quantum teleportation. His concept of "logical depth" — the time required to compute a string from its shortest description — is an important complexity measure.

## Key Works
- Logical depth as a complexity measure
- BB84 quantum key distribution protocol

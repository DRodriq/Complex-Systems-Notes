# Mark A. Bedau

> Philosopher at Reed College who works on emergence, artificial life, and the philosophy of chemistry and synthetic biology.

## Role and Contributions
Bedau's philosophical contributions center on the analysis of emergence, distinguishing weak emergence (deducible but only by simulation) from strong emergence (irreducible to lower-level description). He is also deeply engaged with artificial life as a philosophical program and has written on the ethical and philosophical implications of creating living systems in the laboratory.

# Steen Rasmussen

> Danish physicist and complexity scientist at the Center for Fundamental Living Technology (FLinT) known for work on the origin of life, protocells, and artificial life.

## Role and Contributions
Rasmussen, based at the University of Southern Denmark, has worked on the origin of life problem through the lens of physics and computation. He contributed to Artificial Life II and has developed protocell models — minimal living systems built from scratch in the laboratory. His work bridges theoretical physics, chemistry, and artificial life.

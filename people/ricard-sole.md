# Ricard V. Solé

> Spanish theoretical biologist and complexity scientist at UPF Barcelona who works on ecological complexity, synthetic biology, and the evolution of language and cognition.

## Role and Contributions
Solé applies complex systems methods to ecology (food webs, extinction dynamics, ecological networks), to synthetic biology (designing living machines), and to the evolution of complex structures including language. He co-authored the definitive book on self-organization in complex ecosystems with Bascompte and has published extensively on critical transitions in ecosystems.

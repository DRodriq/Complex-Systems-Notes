# Petter Holme

> Swedish physicist at Aalto University (previously Sungkyunkwan, Kobe), one of the central figures in the development of temporal network theory.

## Role and Contributions
Holme co-authored (with Jari Saramäki) the field-defining 2012 Physics Reports review *Temporal Networks*, which consolidated a fragmented literature into a coherent subfield. His work spans time-respecting paths, burstiness, temporal motifs, and temporal-network epidemic models.

# Donella Meadows

> Systems thinker, environmental scientist, and author (1941–2001) known for co-authoring The Limits to Growth and for Thinking in Systems.

## Role and Contributions
Meadows was a systems dynamics practitioner and environmental scientist at Dartmouth who used system dynamics modeling — developed by Jay Forrester — to analyze global resource and population dynamics. Her "Limits to Growth" (1972) was a landmark systems analysis of planetary limits. Her posthumously published "Thinking in Systems" is the best accessible introduction to systems dynamics thinking.

## Key Works
- The Limits to Growth (1972, with Randers, Meadows)
- Thinking in Systems (2008, posthumous)

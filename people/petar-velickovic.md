# Petar Veličković

> Research scientist at Google DeepMind and affiliated lecturer at the University of Cambridge; a central figure in graph representation learning and geometric deep learning. Co-author of the *Categorical Deep Learning* position paper bringing ACT into mainstream architecture theory.

## Key Works

- [[sources/papers/gavranovic-categorical-deep-learning-2024]]

## Connections

His geometric-deep-learning programme (architectures as group-equivariant maps) is the ML-side lineage that *Categorical Deep Learning* generalizes to arbitrary categorical structure. The DeepMind-inside authorship is part of why that paper matters for [[concepts/category-theory/categorical-cybernetics]] as evidence of ACT reaching the ML mainstream; collaborator with [[paul-lessard]] and [[bruno-gavranovic]].

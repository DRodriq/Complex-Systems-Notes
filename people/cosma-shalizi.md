# Cosma Shalizi

> Statistician and complexity scientist at Carnegie Mellon University known for work on causal states, computational mechanics, and statistical inference for complex systems.

## Role and Contributions
Shalizi, with James Crutchfield, developed computational mechanics — a framework for automatically discovering the causal structure of dynamical systems from their behavior. He is also known for rigorous statistical analysis of complex systems, including influential work critiquing naive power-law fitting. His blog "Three-Toed Sloth" is a landmark in public scientific writing on statistics and complexity.

## Key Works
- Computational mechanics (with Crutchfield)
- Power law fitting and statistical methodology papers

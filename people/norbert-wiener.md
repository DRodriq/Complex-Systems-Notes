# Norbert Wiener

> American mathematician (1894–1964) who founded cybernetics — the study of control and communication in animals and machines — anticipating many ideas in complex systems science.

## Role and Contributions
Wiener's Cybernetics (1948) established the study of feedback, control, and communication as a unified science applicable to both biological and mechanical systems. He introduced the concepts of negative feedback, circular causality, and homeostasis in a mathematical framework. Cybernetics was a direct precursor to systems theory, complexity science, and cognitive science.

## Key Works
- Cybernetics: Or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine (1948)
- The Human Use of Human Beings (1950)

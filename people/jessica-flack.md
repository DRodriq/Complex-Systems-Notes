# Jessica Flack

> Complexity scientist at SFI whose research focuses on collective computation, information processing in biological and social systems, and the evolution of intelligence.

## Role and Contributions
Flack is a professor at SFI and leads the Collective Computation group. Her empirical work on pigtailed macaque social systems revealed how animals collectively compute information about social power structures and use this to regulate conflict. More broadly, she develops theory about how biological systems process information, make decisions, and evolve complexity — connecting dynamical systems, information theory, and evolutionary biology.

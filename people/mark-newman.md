# Mark Newman

> Physicist and network scientist at the University of Michigan who has made major contributions to community detection, spreading processes on networks, and network statistics.

## Role and Contributions
Newman is one of the most influential network scientists, contributing algorithms for community detection (Newman-Girvan modularity), spectral methods for network analysis, models of network growth, and the statistical mechanics of networks. His textbook "Networks: An Introduction" is the definitive reference in the field. He has also studied social networks empirically and developed models of disease spreading on networks.

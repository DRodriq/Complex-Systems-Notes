# Ilya Prigogine

> Belgian-Russian physical chemist (1917–2003) and Nobel laureate who developed the theory of dissipative structures and argued for irreversibility as a fundamental feature of nature.

## Role and Contributions
Prigogine received the 1977 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on non-equilibrium thermodynamics and dissipative structures. He showed that systems driven far from thermodynamic equilibrium can spontaneously develop ordered, organized structures maintained by continuous energy flow. His later work argued that irreversibility and the arrow of time are fundamental, not emergent, features of physical law. He was one of the most philosophically ambitious scientists of the 20th century.

# Antoine Cully

> Robotics researcher and evolutionary computation specialist known for developing quality-diversity algorithms and their application to adaptive robotics.

## Role and Contributions
Cully, based at Imperial College London, is a leading researcher in quality-diversity (QD) optimization — algorithms that find diverse collections of high-performing solutions rather than a single optimum. His Intelligent Trial and Error (ITE) algorithm enables robots to rapidly adapt to damage by searching a pre-computed behavioral repertoire. His work bridges evolutionary computation, robotics, and complex adaptive systems.

# Manfredo do Carmo

> Brazilian mathematician (1928–2018) at IMPA (Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada), Rio de Janeiro, leading figure in differential geometry in Latin America.

## Role and Contributions
Author of *Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces* (1976) and *Riemannian Geometry* (1988, English 1992) — both widely-used graduate textbooks. His research focused on minimal surfaces and their geometric properties.

# Cliff A. Hooker

> Australian philosopher of science who has written extensively on the philosophy of complex systems, reduction, and emergence.

## Role and Contributions
Hooker has contributed to the philosophical foundations of complex systems science, editing the Handbook of the Philosophy of Science volume on complex systems and writing on reduction, emergence, and the epistemological challenges of complexity. He is an advocate for taking complexity seriously as a challenge to traditional scientific methodology.

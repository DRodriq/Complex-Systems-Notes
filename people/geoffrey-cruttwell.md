# Geoffrey Cruttwell

> Category theorist (Mount Allison University, Canada) working on differential and tangent categories and their use in machine learning. Lead author of *Categorical Foundations of Gradient-Based Learning*, which casts deep-learning components as parametric lenses/optics.

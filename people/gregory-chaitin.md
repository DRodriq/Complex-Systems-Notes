# Gregory Chaitin

> Mathematician who independently developed algorithmic information theory and proved Gödel's incompleteness theorem from an information-theoretic perspective using Omega (the halting probability).

## Role and Contributions
Chaitin, working independently from Kolmogorov and Solomonoff, developed algorithmic information theory (AIT) — the study of the minimum description length of objects. His most famous contribution is Omega (Ω), the halting probability, a specific real number that is well-defined but maximally random — its digits cannot be computed or compressed. He showed this implies a deeper randomness at the heart of mathematics than Gödel's theorem alone suggests.

- The Unknowable (1999)

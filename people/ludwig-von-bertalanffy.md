# Ludwig Von Bertalanffy

> Austrian biologist (1901–1972) who founded General Systems Theory, proposing that systems of all kinds share common principles regardless of their specific content.

## Role and Contributions
Bertalanffy proposed General Systems Theory (GST) as a framework applicable across all sciences — biology, physics, sociology — identifying common principles like hierarchy, feedback, equifinality, and open systems dynamics. GST is the intellectual predecessor to systems dynamics, cybernetics, and modern complex systems science, though it was largely non-mathematical.

## Key Works
- General System Theory (1968)

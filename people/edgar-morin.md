# Edgar Morin

> French philosopher and sociologist (1921–) who developed "general complexity" as a philosophical framework, emphasizing self-organization, recursion, and the limits of reductionist thinking.

## Role and Contributions
Morin's six-volume Method is an ambitious attempt to construct a philosophy adequate to complexity. He distinguishes "restricted complexity" (the formal, computational complexity of Wolfram, Holland, etc.) from "general complexity" — a broader philosophical and epistemological approach that incorporates the observer, acknowledges the limits of knowledge, and insists on the dialogic interplay of order and disorder. Influential in France and Latin America.

## Key Works
- La Méthode (6 vols., 1977–2004)
- Introduction à la pensée complexe

# J. D. Cowan

> Mathematical biophysicist at the University of Chicago who made foundational contributions to the theory of neural networks and cortical dynamics.

## Role and Contributions
Jack Cowan, with Hugh Wilson, developed the Wilson-Cowan equations — a model of excitatory-inhibitory neural population dynamics that is still widely used in theoretical neuroscience. He contributed to the mathematical theory of neural networks at SFI and is a founder of computational neuroscience.

## Key Works
- Wilson-Cowan equations (with Wilson)

# Allison Stanger

> Political scientist and complexity thinker whose work examines democracy, globalization, and the application of complex systems ideas to political economy.

## Role and Contributions
Stanger is a Middlebury College professor and Council on Foreign Relations fellow who applies complexity thinking to questions about democracy, markets, and political institutions. She contributed to the complexity economics volume with Arthur and Beinhocker and has written on the complexities of outsourcing and the gig economy.

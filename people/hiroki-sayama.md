# Hiroki Sayama

> Complex systems scientist at Binghamton University who studies evolution, self-organization, and swarm intelligence, and is known for educational contributions to complexity science.

## Role and Contributions
Sayama directs the Center for Collective Dynamics of Complex Systems at Binghamton and works on evolutionary dynamics, swarm intelligence, and network evolution. He authored the open-access textbook "Introduction to the Modeling and Analysis of Complex Systems," which has become widely used in complexity education and was adopted by the SFI MOOC program.

# Thilo Gross

> German physicist at the University of Oldenburg (HIFMB / Helmholtz Institute for Functional Marine Biodiversity), central figure in adaptive / coevolutionary network theory.

## Role and Contributions
Gross (with Bernd Blasius) wrote the defining 2008 review *Adaptive coevolutionary networks* and developed much of the mathematical machinery for systems in which topology and node dynamics coevolve. Also contributed generalized modeling techniques for dynamical systems with partially unknown structure.

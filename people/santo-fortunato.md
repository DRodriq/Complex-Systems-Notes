# Santo Fortunato

> Italian physicist at Indiana University (Luddy School / Observatory on Social Media), known for foundational work on community detection in networks and for the "science of science."

## Role and Contributions
Fortunato's 2010 Physics Reports review *Community Detection in Graphs* is the standard reference for the field. More recent work applies network methods to the structure and dynamics of scientific research itself.

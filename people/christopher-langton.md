# Christopher Langton

> Computer scientist and complexity researcher who founded the field of Artificial Life and introduced the "edge of chaos" hypothesis.

## Role and Contributions
Langton, working at Los Alamos and later SFI, organized the first Artificial Life conference in 1987 and established ALife as a research field studying life-as-it-could-be through computational and synthetic systems. His most influential contribution is the edge-of-chaos hypothesis: that interesting computation, and life itself, occurs at the phase transition between ordered and chaotic regimes in cellular automaton rule space (parameterized by lambda).

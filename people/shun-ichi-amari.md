# Shun-ichi Amari

> Japanese mathematician and information theorist at RIKEN (Brain Science Institute) and emeritus at the University of Tokyo, founder of the field of information geometry.

## Role and Contributions
Amari's program since the 1980s developed the Riemannian geometry of probability distributions, introduced dual affine connections (the e- and m-connections), and established the natural-gradient method for learning. His *Information Geometry and Its Applications* (2016) is the canonical modern treatment. Also foundational work on neural-network learning dynamics via information geometry.

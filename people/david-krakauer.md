# David Krakauer

> President of the Santa Fe Institute and evolutionary theorist whose research spans information theory, cognitive evolution, and the theory of intelligence in complex systems.

## Role and Contributions
Krakauer, as SFI president since 2015, has been instrumental in shaping the contemporary complexity science agenda. His research addresses how information is stored, processed, and transmitted in biological and social systems — including work on cognitive evolution, the neural basis of behavior, and the information theory of complex adaptive systems. He co-edited the foundational SFI volumes and edits the SFI Press.

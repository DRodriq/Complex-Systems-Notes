# Philip Anderson

> American physicist (1923–2020) and Nobel laureate who wrote "More is Different" — the foundational text for emergence in physics — and participated in SFI's founding.

## Role and Contributions
Anderson received the 1977 Nobel Prize in Physics for his work on condensed matter physics, particularly the theory of localization (Anderson localization) and broken symmetry. His 1972 Science essay "More is Different" argued that each level of complexity in nature has genuinely new laws not reducible to the physics below it — providing the intellectual foundation for complexity science's claim to independence. He participated in SFI's founding economics workshops.

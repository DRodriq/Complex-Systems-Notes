# Heinz von Foerster

> Austrian-American physicist and cybernetician (1911–2002) who developed second-order cybernetics and the concept of the "observing system."

## Role and Contributions
Von Foerster extended cybernetics to include the observer — second-order cybernetics examines how observers construct and are part of the systems they observe. His work on self-organization, eigenforms, and radical constructivism influenced complexity philosophy and cognitive science.

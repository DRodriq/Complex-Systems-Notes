# Eric Bonabeau

> French complexity scientist and entrepreneur known for foundational work on swarm intelligence, ant colony optimization, and self-organization in biological systems.

## Role and Contributions
Bonabeau, working with Guy Theraulaz and Marco Dorigo, developed the theory of swarm intelligence — how simple agents following local rules produce intelligent collective behavior. He co-authored the definitive text on self-organization in biological systems and contributed to ant colony optimization algorithms. He later founded consulting firms applying complexity science to business and strategy.

# Duncan James Watts

> Sociologist and network scientist who co-developed the small-world network model (with Steven Strogatz) and contributed to the empirical study of social networks.

## Role and Contributions
Watts, working with Steven Strogatz at Cornell, introduced the Watts-Strogatz small-world network model (1998), showing that networks with high clustering and short path lengths arise naturally when a small fraction of edges are rewired randomly. He later moved to Microsoft Research and then Penn, conducting large-scale empirical studies of social contagion, information cascades, and social influence.

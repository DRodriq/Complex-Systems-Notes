# Henry S. Horn

> Ecologist at Princeton University known for his work on forest succession, tree geometry, and the adaptive geometry of plant architecture.

## Role and Contributions
Horn studied how the geometry of trees — branch angle, leaf arrangement, crown shape — reflects optimization for light capture in different competitive environments. His book "The Adaptive Geometry of Trees" applied optimization and game-theoretic thinking to plant ecology, an early example of evolutionary ecology thinking.

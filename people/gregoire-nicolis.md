# Grégoire Nicolis

> Belgian physical chemist and mathematical physicist who collaborated with Ilya Prigogine on the theory of dissipative structures and non-equilibrium self-organization.

## Role and Contributions
Nicolis was Prigogine's closest collaborator at the Université Libre de Bruxelles, together developing the mathematical theory of dissipative structures. His contributions include the Brusselator model (a paradigmatic chemical oscillator), bifurcation analysis of chemical systems, and the mathematical treatment of pattern formation in non-equilibrium systems. He co-authored both "Exploring Complexity" and "Self-Organization in Non-Equilibrium Systems."

# Peter Turchin

> Ecologist-turned-historian who founded cliodynamics, applying mathematical population modeling and complex systems methods to the quantitative study of history.

## Role and Contributions
Turchin began as an ecologist studying population dynamics, then applied the same mathematical tools — differential equations, cycles, stability analysis — to historical processes. His cliodynamics framework models the rise and fall of empires through secular cycles, elite overproduction, and social cohesion (asabiya). His Seshat database project is building quantitative historical data to test these models.

# Stuart Kauffman

> Theoretical biologist and complexity scientist at SFI who developed self-organization theory for biology, including NK fitness landscapes, autocatalytic sets, and the edge-of-chaos hypothesis.

## Role and Contributions
Kauffman trained as a physician but became one of the most influential theoretical biologists of the late 20th century. He proposed that self-organization — order arising spontaneously from complex systems — is a fundamental source of biological structure alongside natural selection. His NK model provided a rigorous framework for fitness landscapes. His autocatalytic set theory offers a model for the origin of life. His edge-of-chaos hypothesis argues biological evolution tunes systems to criticality.

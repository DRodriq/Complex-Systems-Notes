# Yasaman Bahri

> Theoretical physicist at Google DeepMind (formerly Google Brain), working on the theoretical foundations of deep learning.

## Role and Contributions
Bahri's work includes contributions to the Neural Tangent Kernel and infinite-width network theory, exact Bayesian correspondences for deep networks, and statistical-physics-informed analyses of training dynamics. Part of the cohort of physicists turning their tools on the foundations of deep learning.

## Connections
[[stat-physics-of-learning]], [[neural-networks]]. Infinite-width / NTK theory is one of the cleaner mathematical windows into large networks.

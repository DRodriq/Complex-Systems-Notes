# Haim Sompolinsky

> Israeli-American theoretical physicist at Harvard (Center for Brain Science) and the Hebrew University, a founding figure in the statistical mechanics of neural networks.

## Role and Contributions
Sompolinsky's work since the 1980s (with Crisanti, Sommers, and others) established much of the foundational theory of randomly-connected recurrent neural networks — including the chaos transition in rate networks, dynamics in random neural networks, and the information capacity of attractor networks. Central figure in the "first wave" of stat-mech-of-NNs that preceded the modern DL theory revival.

## Connections
Prototype figure for [[stat-physics-of-learning]] and theoretical neuroscience. Dynamics in random neural networks is a seed for modern [[neural-networks]] theory.

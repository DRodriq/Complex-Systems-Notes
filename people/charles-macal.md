# Charles M. Macal

> Argonne National Laboratory researcher specializing in agent-based modeling methodology and applications to complex social and energy systems.

## Role and Contributions
Macal is a key figure in applied agent-based modeling, particularly for social and infrastructure systems. He has contributed to ABM methodology (with Michael North, co-developer of NetLogo successor frameworks) and to ABM applications in energy systems, healthcare, and supply chain dynamics. His work emphasizes practical ABM methodology and large-scale simulation.

## Key Works
- Tutorial on agent-based modeling and simulation (with North)

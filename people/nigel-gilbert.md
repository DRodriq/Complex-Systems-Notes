# Nigel Gilbert

> British sociologist and pioneer of agent-based social simulation at the University of Surrey, known for establishing computational sociology as a discipline.

## Role and Contributions
Gilbert was one of the founders of social simulation using agent-based models, co-founding the Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation (JASSS) and writing influential textbooks on simulation in social science. He has advocated for ABM as a core method in sociology and social science research.

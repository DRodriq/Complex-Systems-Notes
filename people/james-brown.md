# James Brown

> Macroecologist at the University of New Mexico who developed macroecology and co-developed the WBE allometric scaling model with Geoffrey West.

## Role and Contributions
Brown pioneered macroecology — the statistical study of patterns in species abundance, body size, and geographic range across scales. With Geoffrey West and Brian Enquist, he co-developed the WBE model explaining quarter-power allometric scaling laws through fractal network geometry. His work established quantitative, mechanistic macroecology.

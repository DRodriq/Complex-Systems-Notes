# Stephen Lynch

> Applied mathematician at Manchester Metropolitan University who works on nonlinear dynamical systems and is known for accessible textbooks with computational implementations.

## Role and Contributions
Lynch writes textbooks bridging dynamical systems theory and computational implementation, including volumes using Mathematica, MATLAB, and Python. His work makes nonlinear dynamics accessible for applied scientists and engineers.

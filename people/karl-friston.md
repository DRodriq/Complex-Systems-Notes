# Karl Friston

> British neuroscientist at University College London (Wellcome Centre for Human Neuroimaging), originator of the free-energy principle and active inference — perhaps the most ambitious living attempt to unify thermodynamics, Bayesian inference, variational principles, and dynamical systems under a single framework for brains and self-organizing systems.

## Role and Contributions
Friston's free-energy principle argues that self-organizing systems must minimize a variational free energy to maintain their existence; active inference extends this into action as well as perception. The framework subsumes Bayesian brain hypotheses, predictive coding, and aspects of statistical mechanics and control theory. Controversial in detail, influential in scope.

# Mark Hansen

> American media theorist at Duke University (Program in Literature / MEMS), working at the intersection of media theory, information theory, phenomenology, and complex systems.

## Role and Contributions
Hansen's *New Philosophy for New Media* (2004), *Bodies in Code* (2006), and *Feed-Forward* (2015) bring complexity-science and information-theoretic concepts into media theory and philosophy of technology. Engages heavily with Gilbert Simondon, Whitehead, and Deleuze.

## Connections
Philosopher-synthesizer bucket, media-theory flavor. Uses [[shannon-entropy]], complexity, and feedback concepts as analytical lenses on digital media and computation.

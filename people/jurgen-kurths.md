# Jürgen Kurths

> German physicist and nonlinear scientist at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research known for synchronization theory, network dynamics, and climate applications.

## Role and Contributions
Kurths has made major contributions to the theory of synchronization in coupled oscillators, recurrence analysis of time series, and network-based approaches to climate dynamics. He edits the journal Chaos and has applied nonlinear dynamics methods broadly — from cardiac dynamics to climate tipping points to power grid stability. The Potsdam Complexity Science Group is associated with his work.

## Key Works
- Synchronization: A Universal Concept in Nonlinear Sciences (with Pikovsky, Rosenblum)

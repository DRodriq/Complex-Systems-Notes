# Eric D. Beinhocker

> Economist and complexity thinker at the Oxford Martin School, known for applying evolutionary and complexity economics to business strategy and policy.

## Role and Contributions
Beinhocker's "The Origin of Wealth" (2006) is the most accessible synthesis of complexity economics, arguing that the economy is an evolutionary complex adaptive system. He applies these ideas to business strategy and economic policy at the Oxford Martin School's Institute for New Economic Thinking. He contributed to the complexity economics volume with Arthur and Stanger.

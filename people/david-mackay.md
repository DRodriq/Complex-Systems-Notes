# David MacKay

> British physicist at the University of Cambridge (Cavendish Laboratory; d. 2016), known for foundational contributions to information theory, Bayesian inference, neural networks, and later for work on sustainable energy.

## Role and Contributions
MacKay's textbook *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms* (2003) unified information theory, probabilistic inference, and machine learning into a single problem-driven treatment; it remains one of the most-cited texts in the field. Earlier research introduced variational Bayesian methods for neural networks. Later served as UK Chief Scientific Advisor for Climate Change and wrote *Sustainable Energy — Without the Hot Air*.

# Simon DeDeo

> Physicist and complexity scientist at Carnegie Mellon and SFI who works on information theory, cultural evolution, social dynamics, and the science of science.

## Role and Contributions
DeDeo applies information theory and statistical mechanics to social and cultural phenomena — studying how ideas spread, how institutions evolve, how scientific communities develop. He has developed information-theoretic tools for analyzing narrative, social hierarchy, and collective decision-making. His work bridges physics, social science, and the humanities.

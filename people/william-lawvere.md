# William Lawvere

> American mathematician (1937–2023); foundational figure in category theory. PhD thesis *Functorial Semantics of Algebraic Theories* (Columbia, 1963) established the "syntax is a category, semantics is a functor" stance under most of contemporary applied category theory. Later work on elementary topos theory and the foundations of mathematics.

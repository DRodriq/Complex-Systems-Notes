# John Protevi

> American philosopher at Louisiana State University, working on political philosophy, philosophy of biology, and philosophy of cognition through a Deleuzian and dynamical-systems lens.

## Role and Contributions
Protevi's *Political Affect* (2009) and *Life, War, Earth* (2013) apply complex-systems and dynamical-systems concepts to political and biological thought. Works in the DeLanda-adjacent tradition of using complexity as analytical philosophy, with stronger engagement with cognitive science than DeLanda.

## Connections
Philosopher-synthesizer bucket. Adjacent to DeLanda and the Deleuzian reading of Prigogine/complexity. Engages 4E cognition (embodied / embedded / enacted / extended).

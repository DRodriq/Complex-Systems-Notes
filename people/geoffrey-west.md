# Geoffrey West

> Theoretical physicist and complexity scientist, former president of SFI, known for deriving universal scaling laws in biology, cities, and companies.

## Role and Contributions
West, a theoretical physicist who spent his career at Los Alamos and SFI (where he was president 2005-2009), discovered that metabolic rates, lifespans, and other biological quantities obey quarter-power scaling laws derivable from the fractal geometry of biological distribution networks. He extended this work to show that cities scale superlinearly (more innovation per capita) while companies scale sublinearly (like organisms). His book Scale (2017) synthesizes this program.

# Robert Axelrod

> Political scientist at the University of Michigan known for the Evolution of Cooperation — the seminal work applying game theory and agent-based models to understand the emergence of cooperation.

## Role and Contributions
Axelrod ran the famous Prisoner's Dilemma tournaments showing that Tit-for-Tat outperforms all other strategies in iterated games, providing a mechanism for the evolution of cooperation without kin selection or centralized authority. He developed agent-based models of norms, culture, and ethnic conflict, advocating for ABM as a third mode of scientific inquiry alongside deduction and induction.

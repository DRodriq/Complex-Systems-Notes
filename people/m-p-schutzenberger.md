# M. P. Schützenberger

> French mathematician and physician (1920–1996) known for contributions to formal language theory, combinatorics, and skeptical views on Darwinian evolution.

## Role and Contributions
Schützenberger made significant contributions to formal language theory (Chomsky-Schützenberger theorem) and algebraic combinatorics. He was notably critical of Darwinian mechanisms as sufficient to explain biological complexity, arguing mathematically that the probability of evolving functional proteins was negligible — a controversial position.

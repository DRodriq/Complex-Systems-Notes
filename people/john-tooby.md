# John Tooby

> Anthropologist and evolutionary psychologist at UC Santa Barbara who co-founded evolutionary psychology with Leda Cosmides.

## Role and Contributions
Tooby, with Leda Cosmides, established evolutionary psychology as a research program applying Darwinian natural selection to understanding the design of the human mind. He contributed to SFI workshops on the evolution of cognition and social behavior, connecting evolutionary biology, cognitive science, and complexity.

## Key Works
- The Adapted Mind (with Cosmides and Barkow, 1992)

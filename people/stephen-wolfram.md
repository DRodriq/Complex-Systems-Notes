# Stephen Wolfram

> British-American physicist, computer scientist, and entrepreneur who developed Mathematica and Wolfram|Alpha, and who argues in A New Kind of Science that computation is the fundamental language of nature.

## Role and Contributions
Wolfram made early contributions to cellular automaton theory at SFI and Caltech before founding Wolfram Research (makers of Mathematica). His massive, controversial "A New Kind of Science" (2002) argues that simple computational rules — especially cellular automata — underlie all natural complexity. He is also advancing the Wolfram Physics Project, attempting to derive physics from computational graph rewriting.

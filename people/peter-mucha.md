# Peter J. Mucha

> Applied mathematician at Dartmouth (formerly UNC) who works on network science, community detection, and multilayer networks.

## Role and Contributions
Mucha contributed to community detection in multilayer and temporal networks, particularly the generalized modularity framework. His work extends community structure methods to time-varying networks and networks with multiple types of connections.

# Albert-László Barabási

> Network scientist and physicist who established scale-free networks and preferential attachment as fundamental principles of complex network structure.

## Role and Contributions
Barabási, working at Notre Dame and later Northeastern, discovered that many real-world networks — the web, citation networks, metabolic networks — exhibit power-law degree distributions, which he termed "scale-free." With Réka Albert, he showed this arises from growth plus preferential attachment. His work transformed network science from a branch of mathematics into a major empirical science with broad applicability.

# Surya Ganguli

> American theoretical physicist / neuroscientist at Stanford (Applied Physics and, by courtesy, Neurobiology), working at the intersection of statistical mechanics, theoretical neuroscience, and the foundations of deep learning.

## Role and Contributions
Ganguli's group has produced influential work on the statistical physics of high-dimensional learning dynamics, random matrix theory for neural networks, information-theoretic limits of neural computation, and the geometry of loss landscapes. He is one of the clearest living embodiments of the stat-physicist-turned-DL-theorist archetype.

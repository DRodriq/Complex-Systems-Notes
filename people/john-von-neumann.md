# John von Neumann

> Hungarian-American mathematician (1903–1957) of extraordinary breadth who made foundational contributions to computer architecture, game theory, quantum mechanics, and self-replicating automata.

## Role and Contributions
Von Neumann invented the stored-program computer architecture (von Neumann architecture), laid the mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics, co-founded game theory with Morgenstern, and developed the theory of self-replicating automata — the direct precursor to cellular automata and artificial life. His universal constructor proved that self-replication is computationally possible and set the stage for Langton and the ALife program.

## Key Works
- Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata (1966, posthumous)
- Theory of Games and Economic Behavior (with Morgenstern, 1944)

# James Sethna

> American theoretical physicist at Cornell (Laboratory of Atomic and Solid State Physics), working on statistical mechanics of complex systems with applications to condensed matter, biology, and information processing.

## Role and Contributions
Sethna's *Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity* (2006; 2nd ed 2021) is a widely-used statistical mechanics textbook pitched at complex-systems audiences as much as at physicists. Research spans crackling noise, avalanches, sloppy models in systems biology, and universality in driven disordered systems.

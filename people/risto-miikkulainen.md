# Risto Miikkulainen

> Computer scientist at UT Austin known for neuroevolution, including co-inventing NEAT with Kenneth Stanley, and for work on computational neuroscience and language modeling.

## Role and Contributions
Miikkulainen has worked on neuroevolution, developing NEAT with Kenneth Stanley and earlier work on evolving recurrent networks. He has also contributed to computational models of language and memory, and more recently to neural architecture search and AutoML. His work spans the intersection of neural networks and evolutionary computation.

# Harvey Friedman

> American mathematical logician at Ohio State University known for reverse mathematics and for discovering natural mathematical statements that are independent of standard axiom systems.

## Role and Contributions
Friedman developed reverse mathematics — determining exactly which axioms are needed to prove various mathematical theorems — and famously discovered "Borel determinacy" and other theorems that require strong set-theoretic axioms. His work extends Gödel's incompleteness results in concrete mathematical directions.

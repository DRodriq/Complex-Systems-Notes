# Andy Clark

> British philosopher of mind at the University of Sussex (Centre for Cognitive Science), one of the principal contemporary figures in philosophy of cognition and the extended / predictive-processing traditions.

## Role and Contributions
Clark's *Being There* (1997), *Supersizing the Mind* (2008), *Surfing Uncertainty* (2016), and *The Experience Machine* (2023) develop embodied and predictive-processing accounts of cognition. Bridges Friston's free-energy work, dynamical-systems approaches to cognition, and analytic philosophy of mind. Closer to technical substance than DeLanda/Latour while still operating as philosophy.

## Connections
Bridge figure between the philosopher-synthesizer bucket and the [[stat-physics-of-learning]] / active-inference technical crowd (Friston). Active inference reads across into philosophy through Clark.

# Jordi Bascompte

> Theoretical ecologist at the University of Zurich known for network approaches to biodiversity, mutualistic networks, and ecological resilience.

## Role and Contributions
Bascompte applies network theory to ecology, particularly to mutualistic interaction networks (plant-pollinator, plant-frugivore). His work has shown that the nested architecture of mutualistic networks contributes to biodiversity and resilience. He has also contributed to understanding tipping points and regime shifts in ecological systems.

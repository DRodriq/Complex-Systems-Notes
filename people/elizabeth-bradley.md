# Elizabeth Bradley

> Computer scientist at the University of Colorado who works on nonlinear time series analysis, computational topology, and the use of dynamical systems theory in data science.

## Role and Contributions
Bradley has contributed to methods for analyzing nonlinear time series, applying tools from dynamical systems theory (delay embeddings, attractor reconstruction, topology) to real-world data. She is also known for pedagogical contributions to chaos and nonlinear dynamics and for work bridging dynamical systems theory and machine learning.

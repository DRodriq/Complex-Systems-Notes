# Anil Hirani

> Applied mathematician at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign; foundational figure in **Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC)**. PhD thesis at Caltech (2003, advised by [[jerrold-marsden]]) established DEC as a discrete reformulation of differential geometry and the calculus of differential forms suitable for computational use on simplicial complexes. Co-author of the influential 2005 expository paper with Desbrun, Leok, and Marsden.

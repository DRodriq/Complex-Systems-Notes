# Frans de Waal

> Dutch primatologist (1948–2024) known for his research on social behavior, empathy, and morality in non-human primates, particularly chimpanzees and bonobos.

## Role and Contributions
De Waal spent decades at Emory University studying primate social behavior, documenting empathy, reconciliation, fairness, and political behavior in apes. His work challenged the view that morality is uniquely human and provided empirical grounding for evolutionary ethics. He collaborated with Jessica Flack on the evolutionary origins of moral behavior.

# Jari Saramäki

> Finnish physicist at Aalto University, co-founder (with Petter Holme) of the modern temporal-networks subfield.

## Role and Contributions
Saramäki's work has spanned mobile-phone call networks, social-network dynamics, temporal motifs, and the interplay of topology and timing in contagion. Co-authored the 2012 Physics Reports *Temporal Networks* review that defined the subfield.

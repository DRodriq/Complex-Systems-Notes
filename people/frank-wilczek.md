# Frank Wilczek

> American physicist and Nobel laureate known for the discovery of asymptotic freedom in quantum chromodynamics and for broad philosophical writing on physics and complexity.

## Role and Contributions
Wilczek received the 2004 Nobel Prize in Physics for asymptotic freedom (the property that quarks interact more weakly at high energies). He has written broadly on the philosophical implications of physics for complexity, beauty, and the nature of reality. He proposed the concept of "anyons" (quasiparticles in 2D systems) and has written on time crystals.

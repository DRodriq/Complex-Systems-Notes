# Jaegwon Kim

> Korean-American philosopher (1934–2019) known for his work on supervenience, emergence, and the philosophy of mind, particularly the exclusion problem for mental causation.

## Role and Contributions
Kim developed rigorous accounts of supervenience — the dependence of higher-level properties on lower-level physical properties — and posed the exclusion problem: if physical events are causally sufficient for their effects, how can higher-level (mental, social) events be genuinely causal? His work challenged emergentists and is essential background for the philosophy of emergence in complex systems.

## Key Works
- Supervenience and Mind (1993)
- Mind in a Physical World (1998)

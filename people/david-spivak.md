# David Spivak

> American mathematician at the Topos Institute (Berkeley); central figure in applied category theory. Work spans the operad of wiring diagrams, ACSets/Catlab, polynomial functors as a theory of interaction, and the *Seven Sketches in Compositionality* exposition with Fong.

# P. M. Allen

> Peter Allen is a complexity scientist at Cranfield University known for applying complex systems and evolutionary modeling to urban, economic, and ecological systems.

## Role and Contributions
Allen developed evolutionary models of urban and regional dynamics, applying non-equilibrium thermodynamics and complexity to questions of how cities and economic systems develop. He worked with Prigogine on applications of dissipative structure theory to social systems.

# Melanie Mitchell

> Computer scientist and complexity researcher at SFI and Portland State University known for work on genetic algorithms, cellular automata, analogy-making, and AI.

## Role and Contributions
Mitchell, a student of Douglas Hofstadter, wrote "An Introduction to Genetic Algorithms" (1998), one of the field's best textbooks, and co-authored influential work on evolving cellular automata to perform computation. Her book "Complexity: A Guided Tour" (2009) is the best general introduction to complexity science. At SFI, she has worked on AI, analogy-making (Copycat), and the conceptual foundations of complexity.

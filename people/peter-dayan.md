# Peter Dayan

> British computational neuroscientist, director at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics (Tübingen), previously at UCL Gatsby.

## Role and Contributions
Co-author (with Larry Abbott) of *Theoretical Neuroscience* (2001). Major contributions to reinforcement-learning theory in the brain (the dopamine reward-prediction-error hypothesis with Wolfram Schultz and Read Montague), Bayesian models of perception, and neural coding.

## Connections
Bridges [[reinforcement-learning]] theory, Bayesian inference, and neural coding. One of the principal figures in the UCL Gatsby lineage of computational neuroscience.

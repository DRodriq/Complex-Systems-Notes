# John R. Taylor

> American physicist, emeritus at the University of Colorado Boulder, known for widely-used undergraduate and intermediate physics textbooks.

## Role and Contributions
Author of *Classical Mechanics* (2005) — a standard intermediate text that bridges Newtonian mechanics to Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formulations with clarity and physical motivation. Also authored *An Introduction to Error Analysis*, a pedagogical classic.

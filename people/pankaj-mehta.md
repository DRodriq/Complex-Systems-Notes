# Pankaj Mehta

> American statistical physicist at Boston University, working on the statistical physics of learning, biological information processing, and complex living systems.

## Role and Contributions
Lead author of the widely-read pedagogical review *A high-bias, low-variance introduction to Machine Learning for physicists* (Physics Reports 2019), which frames ML through the lens of statistical mechanics. Broader work spans quantitative cell biology, evolutionary dynamics, and the physics of information in living systems.

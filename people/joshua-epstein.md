# Joshua Epstein

> Complexity scientist and agent-based modeler at NYU known for pioneering agent-based social science, including the Sugarscape model and work on epidemic modeling.

## Role and Contributions
Epstein, with Robert Axtell, developed Sugarscape — one of the first large-scale agent-based social simulations, demonstrating that complex social phenomena (wealth distribution, trade, conflict) emerge from simple agent rules. His "Generative Social Science" manifesto argues that ABM is a new mode of scientific explanation: to explain a social phenomenon, grow it from the bottom up. He has also built influential epidemic ABMs used in policy.

## Key Works
- Growing Artificial Societies (with Axtell, 1996)
- Generative Social Science (2006)

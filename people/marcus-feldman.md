# Marcus W. Feldman

> Population geneticist at Stanford who works on cultural evolution, gene-culture coevolution, and the evolution of social behavior.

## Role and Contributions
Feldman, with Luigi Cavalli-Sforza and later Robert Boyd and Peter Richerson, developed the mathematical theory of cultural evolution and gene-culture coevolution. His models treat cultural transmission as analogous to but distinct from genetic transmission, enabling rigorous study of how cultural traits spread, change, and coevolve with genes.

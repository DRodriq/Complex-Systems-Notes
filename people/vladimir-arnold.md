# Vladimir Arnold

> Russian mathematician (1937–2010), one of the giants of 20th-century dynamical-systems and mathematical-physics; student of Kolmogorov and one of the central figures of the Moscow mathematical school.

## Role and Contributions
Arnold's contributions span the Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser (KAM) theorem on stability of quasi-periodic motion, singularity theory, symplectic geometry, hydrodynamics, and classical mechanics. His textbook *Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics* (1978/1989) is a canonical treatment pairing classical mechanics with differential geometry. He was a forceful advocate for the geometric / intuitive tradition in mathematics and a critic of excessive formalism.

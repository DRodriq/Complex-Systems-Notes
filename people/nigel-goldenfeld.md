# Nigel Goldenfeld

> British-American physicist, Chancellor's Distinguished Professor at UC San Diego (formerly Swanlund Chair at UIUC), known for renormalization-group theory and its applications across condensed matter, turbulence, and biology.

## Role and Contributions
Author of *Lectures on Phase Transitions and the Renormalization Group* (1992), a canonical graduate text that introduced a generation of physicists to RG as a general-purpose tool for coarse-graining and universality. Research spans pattern formation, turbulence, and the statistical physics of evolution and ecosystems. Co-founded the Institute for Universal Biology; prominent voice on applying physics methods to living systems.

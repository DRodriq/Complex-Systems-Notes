# Renaud Lambiotte

> Belgian mathematician and network scientist at Oxford who works on community detection, dynamical processes on networks, and higher-order network structure.

## Role and Contributions
Lambiotte has contributed to community detection (particularly Markov Stability, a flow-based method for multi-resolution community structure), dynamics on networks, and more recently higher-order interactions (simplicial complexes and hypergraphs). He co-authored the Modularity and Dynamics textbook with Michael Schaub.

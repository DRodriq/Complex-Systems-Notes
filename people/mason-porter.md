# Mason Porter

> Applied mathematician at UCLA who works on network science, dynamical systems on networks, and mathematical aspects of complex systems.

## Role and Contributions
Porter works on community detection in networks, multilayer networks, opinion dynamics, and applications of topology to data analysis. He has contributed extensively to the mathematical foundations of network science and to applications in social and biological networks.

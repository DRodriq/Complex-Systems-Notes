# Étienne Ghys

> French mathematician at ENS Lyon, working at the intersection of dynamical systems, geometry, and foliation theory.

## Role and Contributions
Ghys's work spans foliation theory, geometric group theory, complex dynamics, and exceptional expository writing connecting geometry and dynamics for broader audiences. Permanent secretary of the French Academy of Sciences.

## Connections
Dynamics–geometry bridge: prototype modern figure in the tradition connecting [[chaos-theory]], [[differential-geometry]], and group actions on manifolds.

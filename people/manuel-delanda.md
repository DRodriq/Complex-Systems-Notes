# Manuel DeLanda

> Mexican-American philosopher and media theorist based in New York, teaching at Columbia GSAPP (architecture) and the European Graduate School; self-taught in complexity science, nonlinear dynamics, and Deleuzian philosophy.

## Role and Contributions
DeLanda is one of the most visible examples of a deep-autodidact synthesizer: he absorbed stat mech, nonlinear dynamics, Prigogine, Kauffman, and Brian Arthur alongside Deleuze and built a body of philosophical work applying complexity concepts to history, cities, economics, and social theory. Key works: *War in the Age of Intelligent Machines* (1991), *A Thousand Years of Nonlinear History* (1997), *Intensive Science and Virtual Philosophy* (2002), *A New Philosophy of Society* (2006), *Philosophy and Simulation* (2011), *Assemblage Theory* (2016). Influential in architecture and STS; treats complexity science as a conceptual toolkit rather than a predictive apparatus.

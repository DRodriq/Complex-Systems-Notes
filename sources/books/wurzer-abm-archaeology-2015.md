# Agent-based Modeling and Simulation in Archaeology — Wurzer, Kowarik, Reschreiter (2015)

> An edited volume covering agent-based modeling methods and case studies applied to archaeological research problems.

## Orientation
This collection presents both methodological discussions and empirical case studies of ABM applied in archaeological contexts. It addresses how simulation can inform understanding of ancient settlement patterns, resource use, and social organization. Complements the SFI Press volume with a more European perspective and different case studies.

## Key Ideas
- ABM for modeling past human behavioral decisions and their aggregate outcomes
- Methodological debates about validation in historical simulation
- Case studies spanning Bronze Age mining, Roman settlements, and prehistoric foraging

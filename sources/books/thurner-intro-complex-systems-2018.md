# Introduction to the Theory of Complex Systems — Thurner (2018)

> A rigorous theoretical introduction to complex systems from a statistical physics and information-theoretic perspective, co-authored by Stefan Thurner with Rudolf Hanel and Peter Klimek.

## Orientation
Thurner's book approaches complex systems through the lens of generalized statistical mechanics, entropy, and information theory. It extends classical statistical physics to handle systems with long-range correlations, hierarchical structure, and non-ergodic behavior. The treatment is mathematically sophisticated, aimed at physicists and mathematicians entering complexity science.

## Key Ideas
- Generalized entropy: extensions of Boltzmann-Gibbs entropy for complex systems
- Non-extensive statistical mechanics (Tsallis entropy) and its applications
- Networks as substrates for complex systems dynamics
- Scaling and universality classes in complex systems
- Information-theoretic approaches to measuring complexity

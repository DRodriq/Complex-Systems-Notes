# Random Dynamical Systems in Finance — (2013)

> A technical volume applying random dynamical systems theory to financial modeling, connecting stochastic processes and ergodic theory to financial mathematics.

## Orientation
This volume applies the mathematical framework of random dynamical systems — systems driven by random noise but analyzed geometrically — to questions in mathematical finance. It connects ergodic theory, Lyapunov exponents, and invariant measures to financial risk and pricing problems.

## Key Ideas
- Random dynamical systems as the appropriate framework for noise-driven financial processes
- Lyapunov exponents and stability in stochastic financial models
- Ergodic theory for long-run average behavior in markets
- Connections to option pricing and risk assessment

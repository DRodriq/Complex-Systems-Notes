# Differential Equations, Dynamical Systems, and an Introduction to Chaos — Hirsch, Devaney, Smale (2003)

> A rigorous graduate-level textbook on ordinary differential equations and dynamical systems, including an introduction to chaos theory.

## Orientation
This classic text by Hirsch, Devaney, and Smale covers the full mathematical machinery of dynamical systems: linear systems, phase portraits, stability theory, bifurcations, and the transition to chaos. Stephen Smale's influence is evident in the geometric approach. The book is a standard graduate reference in applied mathematics and physics programs.

## Key Ideas
- Linear systems and classification of equilibria via eigenvalue analysis
- Nonlinear systems: Hartman-Grobman theorem, stable/unstable manifolds
- Global behavior: Poincaré-Bendixson theorem, limit cycles
- Bifurcation theory and routes to chaos
- Symbolic dynamics and the mathematical structure of chaos

# Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos: With Applications to Physics, Biology, Chemistry, and Engineering — Strogatz (1994)

> The definitive introductory textbook on nonlinear dynamics and chaos, celebrated for its clarity and breadth of application across the natural sciences.

## Orientation
Strogatz's textbook is the most widely used introduction to nonlinear dynamics and chaos, covering one- and two-dimensional flows, bifurcation theory, limit cycles, and three-dimensional chaos (Lorenz equations). The applications span physics, biology, chemistry, and engineering, and the geometric/intuitive approach makes the material accessible without sacrificing rigor. The vault already has detailed reading notes from this book.

## Key Ideas
- Geometric approach to dynamical systems: phase portraits, fixed points, and stability
- Bifurcation theory: saddle-node, transcritical, pitchfork, and Hopf bifurcations
- Limit cycles and the Poincaré-Bendixson theorem
- Lorenz equations as the paradigmatic chaotic system
- One-dimensional maps: period-doubling, universality, and Feigenbaum constants

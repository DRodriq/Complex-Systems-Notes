# Introduction to Thermodynamics of Irreversible Processes — Prigogine (1961)

> Prigogine's foundational technical work establishing the thermodynamics of irreversible processes, laying the groundwork for his later theory of dissipative structures.

## Orientation
This early technical monograph by Prigogine develops the theoretical framework for systems far from thermodynamic equilibrium. It establishes minimum entropy production principles near equilibrium and sets up the conceptual apparatus — entropy production, thermodynamic forces and fluxes — that Prigogine later extended to the far-from-equilibrium regime where dissipative structures appear.

## Key Ideas
- Entropy production as the fundamental quantity for irreversible processes
- Onsager reciprocal relations connecting thermodynamic forces and fluxes
- Minimum entropy production theorem for near-equilibrium steady states
- Distinction between equilibrium and non-equilibrium thermodynamics
- Precursor to the theory of dissipative structures

# Mathematics in Population Biology and Epidemiology — Castillo-Chavez, Brauer (2001)

> A graduate-level textbook on mathematical epidemiology and population biology, with rigorous analysis of disease transmission models and population dynamics.

## Orientation
This textbook by Castillo-Chavez and Brauer covers the mathematical theory of infectious disease spread, from basic SIR compartmental models to more complex frameworks with age structure, spatial spread, and multiple pathogen strains. It provides the mathematical tools (stability analysis, threshold theorems) central to theoretical epidemiology.

## Key Ideas
- SIR and SEIR models as the foundation of compartmental epidemiology
- Basic reproduction number R0 and the epidemic threshold
- Age-structured population models
- Multi-patch models for spatially heterogeneous populations
- Stability and persistence of endemic equilibria

# Seven Sketches in Compositionality — Fong & Spivak (2018)

> The accessible entry point to applied category theory. Seven self-contained "sketches" covering orders and Galois connections, databases as functors, signal flow as monoidal categories, co-design as enriched profunctors, signal processing, electric circuits, and logic of behaviour.

## Orientation

Originally published on arXiv as 1803.05316 (2018), then as a book by Cambridge UP (2019). Designed as a textbook for the **Applied Category Theory** community and used widely in the **Adjoint School**. Chapter 1 introduces [[generative-effects]] via the connected-components functor example — the canonical illustration of a functor failing to preserve joins.

## Key Ideas

- Generative effects: $F(a \vee b) \neq F(a) \vee F(b)$
- Galois connections and adjoint functors as compositional structure
- Databases and schemas as categories
- Wiring diagrams, monoidal categories, profunctors
- Functorial semantics throughout

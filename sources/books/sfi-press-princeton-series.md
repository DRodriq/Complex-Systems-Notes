# Princeton Studies in Complexity (Series) — SFI Press

> The Princeton Studies in Complexity series, published in collaboration with Princeton University Press and the Santa Fe Institute, presenting foundational and cutting-edge research in complexity science.

## Orientation
This book series published many of the canonical works in complexity science during the 1990s and 2000s, including volumes on networks, evolution, economics, and computation. It represents the institutional publishing output of the SFI program during its most productive period.

## Key Ideas
- Series covers all major subfields of complexity science
- Volumes include technical research monographs and accessible syntheses
- Represents the SFI intellectual program in book form

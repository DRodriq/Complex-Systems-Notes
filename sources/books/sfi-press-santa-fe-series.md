# Santa Fe Institute Series — SFI Press

> The Santa Fe Institute book series, encompassing both the Princeton Studies in Complexity and the SFI Press imprints, covering the full range of complexity science research.

## Orientation
The Santa Fe Institute has published books through multiple channels, including early collaborations with Addison-Wesley and Princeton University Press, as well as its own SFI Press imprint. This series entry represents the broader SFI publishing tradition. Key volumes include the Artificial Life proceedings, Economy as an Evolving Complex System, and Worlds Hidden in Plain Sight.

## Key Ideas
- Institutional publishing output of SFI's research program
- Spans popular science, graduate textbooks, and research proceedings

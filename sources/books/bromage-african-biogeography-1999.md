# African Biogeography, Climate Change, and Human Evolution — Bromage, Schrenk (1999)

> An edited volume examining the relationships between African biogeography, climate variability, and the evolution of the human lineage.

## Orientation
This volume brings together paleoanthropological, geological, and ecological perspectives on how African climate change and biogeographic change drove human evolutionary dynamics. It is relevant to the vault primarily as an application of complex systems thinking (climate dynamics, ecology, evolution) to human origins.

## Key Ideas
- African climate variability as a driver of hominin speciation and dispersal
- Biogeographic barriers and corridors shaping evolutionary trajectories
- Pulsed climate change (variability selection hypothesis) as evolutionary engine

# Emerging Syntheses in Science: Proceedings of the Founding Workshops of the Santa Fe Institute — SFI Press

> The proceedings from SFI's founding workshops, documenting the early intellectual convergence of physicists, biologists, economists, and computer scientists around complexity science.

## Orientation
This volume captures the founding intellectual moment of the Santa Fe Institute, where figures like Murray Gell-Mann, Philip Anderson, Kenneth Arrow, and others converged to define a new science of complex adaptive systems. It represents the institutional origin point of the modern complexity science enterprise.

## Key Ideas
- Cross-disciplinary identification of common patterns (emergence, self-organization, adaptation)
- Economics, physics, and biology sharing mathematical structures
- Early articulation of complex adaptive systems as a unifying concept
- The SFI model: small resident faculty + rotating visitors producing synthesis

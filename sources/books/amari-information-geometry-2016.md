# Information Geometry and Its Applications (Amari, 2016)

Springer Applied Mathematical Sciences 194. Treats families of probability
distributions as Riemannian manifolds, with the Fisher information metric as
the natural geometry. Covers dual connections, divergence functions, natural
gradient methods, and applications to statistics, ML, and neuroscience.

## Why it's here

Advanced capstone of the mathematical-physics pathway. Ties together
information theory, differential geometry, statistical mechanics, and learning
theory. To be undertaken only after the foundations (Sethna, MacKay, Lee,
do Carmo) are in place.

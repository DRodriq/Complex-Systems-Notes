# Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics (Arnold, 1989)

Springer Graduate Texts in Mathematics 60 (2nd ed, 1989; original Russian 1974).
Famously elegant and mathematically rich. Develops classical mechanics from
a differential-geometric standpoint: configuration space as a manifold,
symplectic structure on phase space, KAM theorem, rigid body dynamics via Lie
groups.

## Why it's here

Capstone text for the classical-mechanics track, to be ground through after
Taylor has established intuition. Bridges directly into the differential-geometry
track (symplectic and Riemannian structures). Arnold was one of the principal
exponents of the visual/geometric tradition in mathematics — his style maps
well onto Dane's modality.

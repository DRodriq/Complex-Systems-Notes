# Artificial Life II — Langton, Farmer, Rasmussen, Taylor (1992)

> The second Artificial Life proceedings volume, expanding the field's scope into coevolution, ecosystems, developmental systems, and the theoretical foundations of life.

## Orientation
Building on the inaugural volume, Artificial Life II deepened the theoretical and empirical investigation of synthetic life. Key contributions covered coevolutionary dynamics, adaptive landscapes, artificial immune systems, and the physics of computation. Doyne Farmer, Steen Rasmussen, and Norman Packard were among the contributors exploring connections to thermodynamics and information theory.

## Key Ideas
- Coevolution as a driver of complexity (Red Queen dynamics)
- Adaptive landscapes and evolutionary search
- Connections between thermodynamics, information, and living systems
- Development and morphogenesis in artificial systems
- Ecological dynamics emerging from agent interactions

# Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: Foundations and Modern Approaches — Albrecht, Christianos, Shafer (2024)

> A comprehensive graduate textbook on multi-agent reinforcement learning, covering theory, algorithms, and applications in cooperative, competitive, and mixed-motive settings.

## Orientation
This textbook covers the full scope of multi-agent RL, from game-theoretic foundations through modern deep MARL algorithms. It addresses cooperative agents, competitive agents, and the emergence of complex collective behavior from learning interactions. The book bridges reinforcement learning, game theory, and complex systems perspectives.

## Key Ideas
- Multi-agent systems as interactive decision-making problems
- Equilibrium concepts in multi-agent settings (Nash, correlated equilibrium)
- Cooperative MARL: credit assignment, communication, and team reward
- Emergent behavior from multi-agent interaction
- Applications in autonomous vehicles, robotics, and economic simulation

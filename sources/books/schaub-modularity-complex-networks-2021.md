# Modularity and Dynamics on Complex Networks — Schaub, Lambiotte (2021)

> A focused technical treatment of modularity and community structure in complex networks, examining how network topology shapes dynamical processes and vice versa.

## Orientation
Schaub and Lambiotte examine the relationship between network modularity — the tendency of networks to organize into quasi-independent modules — and dynamical processes running on those networks. The book covers spectral methods, random walks, and flow-based approaches to community detection, and analyzes how modular structure affects diffusion, synchronization, and spreading dynamics.

## Key Ideas
- Modularity as a structural property shaping network dynamics
- Spectral methods (graph Laplacian, eigenvalue analysis) for community detection
- Random walk perspectives on network structure
- How dynamics on networks reveal community structure
- Applications in neuroscience, social networks, and infrastructure

# Principles of Planetary Climate — Pierrehumbert (2010)

> A rigorous physical treatment of climate science from first principles, covering radiative transfer, atmospheric thermodynamics, and climate dynamics for Earth and other planets.

## Orientation
Pierrehumbert's textbook provides a physically grounded, mathematically rigorous treatment of planetary climates. It covers blackbody radiation, greenhouse effect physics, atmospheric thermodynamics, and the dynamics of planetary atmospheres. The book is both a climate science textbook and a contribution to understanding Earth as a complex physical system.

## Key Ideas
- Radiative transfer and the greenhouse effect from first principles
- Energy balance models of planetary climate
- Atmospheric thermodynamics and the dynamics of weather systems
- Snowball Earth and climate tipping points
- Comparative planetology: Venus, Mars, and exoplanets

# Hidden Order: How Adaptation Builds Complexity — Holland (1995)

> Holland's accessible account of complex adaptive systems, introducing his framework for how adaptation at the agent level produces emergent complexity at the system level.

## Orientation
Holland presents his theoretical framework for complex adaptive systems (CAS) in an accessible form, drawing on his work in genetic algorithms, classifier systems, and agent-based models. The book is centered on the idea that CAS share common mechanisms: flows, tags, nonlinearity, and the building blocks from which novelty emerges. It is the companion volume to his more technical work.

## Key Ideas
- Complex adaptive systems defined by adaptation and emergence
- Tags: markers that enable selective interaction and drive emergent organization
- Flows of resources and information through adaptive networks
- Building blocks: how CAS recombine existing structures to generate novelty
- Nonlinearity as the source of surprising system behavior

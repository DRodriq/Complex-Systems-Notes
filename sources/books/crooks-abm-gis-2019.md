# Agent-Based Modelling & Geographical Information Systems: A Practical Primer — Crooks (2019)

> A practical textbook integrating agent-based modeling with geographic information systems for simulating spatially explicit social and environmental systems.

## Orientation
This primer addresses the growing intersection of ABM and GIS, providing hands-on guidance for building geospatially aware agent-based models. It covers tools, methods, and applied examples in urban dynamics, epidemiology, and land-use change. Aimed at researchers who want to add spatial realism to their simulation work.

## Key Ideas
- Integration of ABM with spatial data and GIS platforms
- Spatially explicit models capture geography-dependent emergent patterns
- Applications in urban growth, disease spread, evacuation modeling
- Practical workflow from data collection to model validation

# Encyclopedia of Complexity and Systems Science — Springer (2009)

> A comprehensive multi-volume reference encyclopedia covering the full breadth of complexity and systems science, from dynamical systems to social simulation.

## Orientation
This encyclopedia spans thousands of articles across all areas of complexity and systems science: chaos, networks, agent-based modeling, information theory, evolutionary dynamics, econophysics, and more. Edited by Robert Meyers, it serves as a reference work rather than a narrative text. Useful for looking up technical definitions and finding entry points into specialized subfields.

## Key Ideas
- Reference coverage of all major concepts in complexity science
- Cross-disciplinary scope connecting physics, biology, economics, and social science
- Technical articles from leading researchers in each subfield

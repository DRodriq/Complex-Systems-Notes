# Introduction to the Modeling and Analysis of Complex Systems — Sayama (2015)

> A comprehensive, freely available textbook introducing complex systems methods — dynamical systems, networks, agent-based models, and cellular automata — with Python implementations.

## Orientation
Sayama's textbook is widely used in complexity science education, covering the full range of methods with Python code examples. It is notable for being openly licensed and used at the Santa Fe Institute's MOOC and many universities. The book bridges theoretical concepts and computational implementation accessibly.

## Key Ideas
- Unified survey of complex systems modeling approaches
- Dynamical systems: ODEs, maps, chaos, and bifurcations
- Network analysis: small worlds, scale-free networks, community structure
- Agent-based modeling from scratch in Python
- Cellular automata and self-organization

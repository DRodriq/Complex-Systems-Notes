# Infinite Dimensional Dynamical Systems — Mallet-Paret (2013)

> A technical mathematical volume on dynamical systems in infinite-dimensional spaces, covering delay differential equations, parabolic PDEs, and related topics.

## Orientation
This is a specialized mathematical research volume dealing with dynamical systems on infinite-dimensional state spaces — arising, for example, from delay differential equations or partial differential equations. It is primarily of interest to mathematicians working on functional analysis and advanced dynamics theory.

## Key Ideas
- Global attractors in infinite-dimensional phase spaces
- Delay differential equations as generators of infinite-dimensional dynamics
- Geometric theory of infinite-dimensional systems

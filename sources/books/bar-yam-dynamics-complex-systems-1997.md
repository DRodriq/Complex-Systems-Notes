# Dynamics of Complex Systems — Bar-Yam (1997)

> A graduate-level textbook developing a unified mathematical framework for complex systems, covering dynamics, pattern formation, neural networks, and evolution through the lens of multi-scale analysis.

## Orientation
Bar-Yam's foundational technical work, written to provide a rigorous quantitative treatment of complex systems. Unlike popular accounts, this book builds from statistical mechanics and dynamical systems toward a general theory of complexity. It is notably broader in scope than most complexity texts, covering biological, physical, and social systems under the same mathematical roof. The book laid the groundwork for Bar-Yam's signature concept of multi-scale analysis.

## Key Ideas
- Complex systems as systems with many interacting components exhibiting emergent collective behavior
- Multi-scale analysis: understanding systems by examining structure and behavior across different spatial and temporal scales
- Connections between neural networks, evolution, and pattern formation as instances of the same underlying dynamics
- Complexity as a quantifiable property of a system's description across scales

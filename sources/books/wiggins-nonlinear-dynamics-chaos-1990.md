# Introduction to Applied Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos — Wiggins (1990)

> A graduate-level textbook on applied nonlinear dynamics, covering the geometric theory of dynamical systems including Melnikov methods, horseshoes, and the mathematical foundations of chaos.

## Orientation
Wiggins' text is more technically demanding than Strogatz, covering Smale horseshoes, Shilnikov phenomena, Melnikov's method for homoclinic bifurcations, and KAM theory. It is aimed at applied mathematicians who need rigorous geometric tools for analyzing chaotic systems. A key reference for researchers going beyond Strogatz.

## Key Ideas
- Smale horseshoe: geometric construction proving chaotic dynamics
- Melnikov's method: analytical tool for detecting homoclinic tangles
- Normally hyperbolic invariant manifolds in higher-dimensional systems
- KAM theory and the persistence of quasi-periodic motion
- Shilnikov homoclinic orbits and their role in creating chaos

# Networks (Newman, 2018, 2nd ed)

Oxford University Press. Canonical modern textbook for network science. Covers
graph theory, random graph models (Erdős–Rényi, configuration, exponential),
degree distributions, clustering, small-world and scale-free phenomena,
centrality, community detection, percolation, epidemics, and dynamical
processes on networks.

Distinct from `newman-barabasi-watts-networks-2006` (earlier collected volume).

## Why it's here

Canonical entry point for the network-science track in the pathway. Installs
the vocabulary and structural toolkit that underpins all downstream temporal,
adaptive, and multilayer network work.

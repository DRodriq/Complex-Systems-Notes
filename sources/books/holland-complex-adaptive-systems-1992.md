# Complex Adaptive Systems — Holland (1992)

> Holland's foundational theoretical framework for complex adaptive systems, presenting the formal mathematical and computational basis for understanding adaptation and emergence.

## Orientation
This work presents Holland's formal framework for complex adaptive systems (CAS), including classifier systems (rule-based adaptive agents), genetic algorithms, and the mathematical theory of schemas. It is more technical than "Hidden Order" and provides the theoretical foundations that underpin much of Holland's subsequent work and the broader SFI CAS research program.

## Key Ideas
- Complex adaptive systems defined formally in terms of adaptation and internal models
- Classifier systems: learning systems that evolve rule sets through genetic algorithms
- Schema theorem: mathematical analysis of genetic algorithm dynamics
- Emergence as the central phenomenon requiring explanation in CAS
- Building blocks: low-order schemas that recombine to produce novelty

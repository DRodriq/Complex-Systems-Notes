# Modeling, Building, Writing: A History of Nonlinear Dynamics and Complex Systems — Williams (2012)

> A historical account of the development of nonlinear dynamics and complex systems science from the late 19th century through the modern era.

## Orientation
Williams traces the intellectual lineage of nonlinear dynamics from Poincaré through the chaos revolution and into complexity science, examining how mathematical and physical ideas evolved alongside computing technology. The book places the field in historical and social context.

## Key Ideas
- Poincaré as the originator of geometric dynamical systems thinking
- Role of computing in enabling exploration of nonlinear phenomena
- Social and institutional dimensions of the chaos and complexity revolutions
- Connections between mathematical traditions across countries and decades

## Vault Relevance
Historical context for [[dynamical systems]] and [[chaos]]; useful background for understanding the intellectual genealogy of complexity science.

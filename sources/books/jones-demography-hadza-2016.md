# Demography and Evolutionary Ecology of Hadza Hunter-Gatherers — Jones (2016)

> A quantitative study of the demography and evolutionary ecology of the Hadza people of Tanzania, one of the last remaining hunter-gatherer populations.

## Orientation
This monograph applies life history theory and demographic modeling to the Hadza, providing detailed data on fertility, mortality, growth, and subsistence. It contributes to understanding human life history evolution and to anthropological models of hunter-gatherer ecology and social organization.

## Key Ideas
- Hadza life history parameters: fertility, survivorship, foraging returns
- Life history theory applied to human hunter-gatherers
- Demographic modeling of small-scale human societies
- Subsistence ecology and its demographic consequences

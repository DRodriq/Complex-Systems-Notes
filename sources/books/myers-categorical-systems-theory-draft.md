# Categorical Systems Theory — Myers (book draft, ongoing)

> Ongoing book draft proposing a **double-categorical** framework for systems theory that unifies the many existing categorical treatments of open dynamical systems (continuous, discrete, stochastic, deterministic) into a single composition framework.

## Orientation

By the late 2010s, categorical approaches to open systems had matured along several distinct lines — structured cospans, the operad of wiring diagrams, the category Poly. Myers' framework uses double categories to provide a common vocabulary in which these approaches can be compared, combined, and extended. The draft is circulated openly in the ACT community; final published form pending.

## Key Ideas

- Double-categorical syntax for systems + system maps
- Unification across continuous, discrete, stochastic, deterministic open systems
- Composition laws made explicit at the 2-cell level
- Behaviours as functors between double categories

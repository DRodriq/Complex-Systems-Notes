# The Complexity of Cooperation — Axelrod (2011)

> Axelrod's follow-up to The Evolution of Cooperation, presenting agent-based models of social phenomena including norms, culture, alliance formation, and ethnic conflict.

## Orientation
Building on his famous cooperation tournaments, Axelrod develops agent-based models of social complexity: how norms emerge and spread, how ethnic identity influences conflict, how alliances form, and how adaptive agents evolve strategies. The book is a methodological manifesto for agent-based social science as well as a series of substantive models.

## Key Ideas
- Agent-based modeling as a third mode of science alongside deduction and induction
- Norm emergence: how individuals enforce social norms without central authority
- Cultural dissemination: how local interaction spreads and differentiates culture
- Ethnic conflict and in-group bias in multi-ethnic societies
- The evolution of strategies in iterated games

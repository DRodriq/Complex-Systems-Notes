# Foundations of Complex Systems — Krakauer, Flack, eds. (2010)

> An edited SFI volume laying out the theoretical foundations of complex systems science, with contributions covering information, computation, evolution, and collective behavior.

## Orientation
This volume, edited by David Krakauer and Jessica Flack, represents SFI's effort to articulate the theoretical core of complexity science. It collects foundational perspectives on what complex systems are, how they compute, how they evolve, and what information means in these contexts. A key reference for the intellectual program of complexity science.

## Key Ideas
- Information processing as a unifying lens for complex systems
- Collective computation: how groups of agents process information collectively
- Evolution as a search process in high-dimensional fitness landscapes
- Robustness and degeneracy as adaptive properties of complex systems
- Formalization of emergence, adaptation, and hierarchy

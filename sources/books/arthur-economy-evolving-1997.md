# The Economy as an Evolving Complex System II — Arthur, Durlauf, Lane (1997)

> The second volume from the SFI economics program, presenting theoretical and empirical advances in complexity economics including evolution, learning, and emergent economic order.

## Orientation
This volume from the SFI Santa Fe Institute economics research program gathers contributions from the 1990s expanding on the foundational ideas from the first volume. It covers computational economics, agent-based economic models, evolutionary game theory, and empirical applications of complexity economic ideas to industrial dynamics and economic history.

## Key Ideas
- Endogenous growth and innovation as emergent properties of economic networks
- Agent-based models of market behavior and asset pricing
- Evolutionary game theory in economic settings
- Learning and adaptation in economic agents
- Self-reinforcing mechanisms in economic geography and industrial location

# Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity (Sethna, 2021)

2nd edition, Oxford University Press. Free online at
https://sethna.lassp.cornell.edu/StatMech/

Statistical mechanics textbook pitched at a complex-systems audience rather than
condensed-matter physicists. Problem-driven. Introduces entropy, ensembles,
free energy, phase transitions, universality, and the renormalization group with
a consistent eye toward applications beyond physics (networks, biology, learning).

## Why it's here

Primary next target for the statistical-mechanics track in the personal
education pathway. Installs ensemble thinking, entropy as a counting argument,
phase transitions, universality, and RG — each a genuinely new organ of thought.
Directly relevant to IE/streaming work (already using entropy on cluster
occupancy; universality and RG bear on multi-scale IE structure).

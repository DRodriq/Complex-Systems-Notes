# Philosophy of Complex Systems — Hooker (2011)

> An edited philosophical volume examining the conceptual foundations and epistemological challenges of complex systems science.

## Orientation
Hooker's volume brings together philosophical analyses of what complex systems science is, what it explains, and how it relates to traditional scientific frameworks. Topics include reduction and emergence, the role of models and simulation in science, causation in complex systems, and the relationship between complexity and evolution.

## Key Ideas
- Emergence as a philosophical challenge to reductionist science
- Simulation as a new mode of scientific explanation
- Causation in non-linear systems: multiple realizability and downward causation
- Complex systems and the limits of prediction and control
- Methodological pluralism as appropriate for complex domains

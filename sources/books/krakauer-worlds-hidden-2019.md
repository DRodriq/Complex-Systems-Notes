# Worlds Hidden in Plain Sight: Thirty Years of Complexity Thinking at SFI — Krakauer, ed. (2019)

> An anthology celebrating thirty years of the Santa Fe Institute, collecting essays and excerpts from major SFI contributors spanning the full range of complexity science.

## Orientation
Edited by SFI president David Krakauer, this volume surveys three decades of complexity thinking at SFI. It includes contributions from Murray Gell-Mann, Stuart Kauffman, Geoffrey West, Jessica Flack, Simon DeDeo, and many others. The book serves as both an intellectual history and a state-of-the-field summary, showing how complexity science has evolved and what its core commitments are.

## Key Ideas
- Complexity science as a unified inquiry into emergent phenomena
- Information as a unifying framework for complexity
- Evolution, adaptation, and selection as universal processes
- Cities, economies, and ecosystems as complex adaptive systems
- The future of complexity science and its applications

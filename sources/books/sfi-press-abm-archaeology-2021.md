# Agent-Based Modeling for Archaeology: Simulating the Complexity of Societies — SFI Press (2021)

> A methodological guide from the Santa Fe Institute applying agent-based modeling techniques to archaeological questions about ancient social complexity.

## Orientation
This volume from SFI Press applies ABM methodology to archaeological research, covering both the technical aspects of building models and the epistemological challenges of using simulation to reason about past societies. It serves as a bridge between computational social science and archaeology.

## Key Ideas
- ABM as a tool for testing hypotheses about past social dynamics
- Modeling emergence of social complexity from simple agent interactions
- Challenges of parameterization and validation in historical simulation
- Case studies from Pueblo societies, ancient trade networks, and settlement patterns

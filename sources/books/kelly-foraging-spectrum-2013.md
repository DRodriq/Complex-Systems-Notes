# The Lifeways of Hunter-Gatherers: The Foraging Spectrum — Kelly (2013)

> Robert Kelly's comprehensive comparative study of hunter-gatherer societies worldwide, analyzing the full range of variation in subsistence, mobility, social organization, and cultural practices.

## Orientation
Kelly's book examines the diversity of foraging societies worldwide, rejecting simple models in favor of a nuanced picture of how environment, demography, and social organization interact. The "foraging spectrum" concept captures the continuum from highly mobile to semi-sedentary, from simple to complex, among hunter-gatherers. It is essential reading in evolutionary anthropology.

## Key Ideas
- The foraging spectrum: variation in mobility, territory, storage, and social complexity
- Environmental and demographic drivers of hunter-gatherer diversity
- Mobility strategies and their social and demographic correlates
- Egalitarianism vs. complexity: what drives stratification in foraging societies

# Classical Mechanics (Taylor, 2005)

University Science Books. Friendly but rigorous entry to Lagrangian and
Hamiltonian mechanics. Builds from Newtonian mechanics through calculus of
variations to phase-space formulations and symmetries/conservation laws.

## Why it's here

Entry point for the classical-mechanics track in the pathway. Installs
variational thinking, phase space as native territory, and Noether's
symmetry↔conservation correspondence — all directly reinforcing the
dynamical-systems foundation from Strogatz.

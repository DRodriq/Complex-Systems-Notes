# Self-Organization in Complex Ecosystems — Sole, Bascompte (2006)

> A rigorous treatment of self-organization and complex dynamics in ecological systems, covering food webs, extinction cascades, biodiversity patterns, and ecological phase transitions.

## Orientation
Sole and Bascompte apply the mathematical tools of complex systems — network theory, phase transitions, self-organized criticality — to ecology. They examine how ecosystems organize themselves, how food webs are structured, what drives extinction cascades, and how biodiversity patterns arise. The book is both a complexity science text and a contribution to theoretical ecology.

## Key Ideas
- Ecological networks (food webs) as complex adaptive systems
- Phase transitions in ecosystems: regime shifts and tipping points
- Self-organized criticality in extinction dynamics
- Biodiversity-stability relationships and network topology
- Spatial ecology and pattern formation in ecosystems

# Network Science (Barabási, 2016)

Cambridge University Press. Free online at http://networksciencebook.com/

Textbook-style treatment of network science with an emphasis on scale-free
networks, preferential attachment, and real-world network phenomena. More
applied and visual than Newman; complementary rather than substitute.

## Why it's here

Secondary reference for the network-science track — complementary perspective
to Newman, with stronger treatment of preferential-attachment dynamics and
empirical network phenomena.

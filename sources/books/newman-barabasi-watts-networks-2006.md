# The Structure and Dynamics of Networks — Newman, Barabasi, Watts (2006)

> An anthology of foundational papers in complex network science, edited by three of the field's leading figures, covering small-world networks, scale-free networks, and network dynamics.

## Orientation
This collection brings together the landmark papers that established network science as a field: Watts-Strogatz small-world networks, Barabasi-Albert scale-free networks, and key papers on network community structure, epidemics on networks, and network resilience. It includes editorial commentary by the three editors situating each paper in context. An essential reference for anyone working with complex networks.

## Key Ideas
- Small-world networks: high clustering with short path lengths (Watts-Strogatz)
- Scale-free networks: power-law degree distributions via preferential attachment (Barabasi-Albert)
- Network community structure and modular organization
- Epidemics and spreading processes on networks
- Robustness and fragility of networks to targeted vs. random failures

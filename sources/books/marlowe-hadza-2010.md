# The Hadza: Hunter-Gatherers of Tanzania — Marlowe (2010)

> Frank Marlowe's comprehensive ethnography of the Hadza people of Tanzania, the most extensively studied contemporary hunter-gatherer society.

## Orientation
Marlowe's monograph is the definitive ethnographic account of Hadza society, covering subsistence strategies, food sharing, social organization, mating patterns, and life history. It provides a rich empirical base for theoretical models of hunter-gatherer social dynamics and human evolutionary ecology.

## Key Ideas
- Hadza subsistence: foraging strategies, food sharing norms, camp organization
- Kinship, marriage, and mating: flexibility and variation in Hadza social organization
- Egalitarianism and conflict avoidance mechanisms
- Comparative data relevant to ancestral human social organization

Peripheral; relevant to evolutionary anthropology and applied complexity threads on human social dynamics.

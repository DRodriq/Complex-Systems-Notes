# Lectures on Phase Transitions and the Renormalization Group (Goldenfeld, 1992)

Addison-Wesley / Frontiers in Physics 85. Authoritative treatment of phase
transitions, critical phenomena, scaling, and the renormalization group. Deeper
on RG than Sethna; less eclectic.

## Why it's here

Optional deeper pass on RG after Sethna. The RG is one of the most powerful
thinking tools in physics — scale-dependent reasoning, universality classes,
fixed points of flow in coupling-constant space — and warrants dedicated
treatment if Sethna's coverage leaves appetite.

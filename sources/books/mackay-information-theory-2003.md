# Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms (MacKay, 2003)

Cambridge University Press. Free online at
https://www.inference.org.uk/itila/

Treats information theory, probabilistic inference, and machine learning as one
integrated subject. Problem-driven, with physical intuition throughout. Known
for exceptional pedagogy.

## Why it's here

Second target in the pathway after Sethna. Shares the entropy concept with
statistical mechanics and bridges to ML via Jaynes's max-entropy principle and
variational inference. Doubles as the holistic "math for AI" treatment —
obviates a separate track.

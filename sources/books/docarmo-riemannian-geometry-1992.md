# Riemannian Geometry (do Carmo, 1992)

Birkhäuser. Classic graduate introduction to Riemannian geometry. Covers
metrics, connections, curvature (sectional, Ricci, scalar), geodesics, Jacobi
fields, and comparison theorems.

## Why it's here

Natural sequel to Lee's smooth manifolds on the differential-geometry track.
Riemannian structure is the substrate for information geometry and for
geometric views of optimization on manifolds (relevant to ML theory).

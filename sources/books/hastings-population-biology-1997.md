# Population Biology: Concepts and Models — Hastings (1997)

> An introductory textbook on population biology that integrates mathematical modeling with ecological concepts, covering single-species dynamics through multi-species interactions.

## Orientation
Hastings' text provides a rigorous but accessible mathematical treatment of population biology, covering logistic growth, competition (Lotka-Volterra), predation, age-structured models, and spatial population dynamics. It balances theory and biological interpretation, making it useful for ecologists with mathematical training.

## Key Ideas
- Single-species models: logistic growth, delay differential equations, cycles
- Multi-species competition and the competitive exclusion principle
- Predator-prey dynamics: cycles, limit cycles, and chaos
- Age-structured models: Leslie matrices and reproductive value
- Spatial models: metapopulations and dispersal

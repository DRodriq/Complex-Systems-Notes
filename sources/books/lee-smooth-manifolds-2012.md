# Introduction to Smooth Manifolds (Lee, 2012, 2nd ed)

Springer Graduate Texts in Mathematics 218. Standard modern entry to smooth
manifold theory. Covers charts, atlases, tangent spaces, vector fields,
differential forms, Lie groups, integration, and de Rham cohomology.

## Why it's here

Entry point for the differential-geometry track, grinding through after
statistical mechanics and classical mechanics have built up the conceptual
motivation (ensemble manifolds, configuration/phase-space manifolds, embedding
manifolds from ML work).

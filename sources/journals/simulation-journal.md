# Simulation: Transactions of The Society for Modeling and Simulation International

> The primary journal of the Society for Modeling and Simulation International, covering simulation methodology and applications.

## Scope
Covers discrete-event simulation, agent-based simulation, continuous simulation, and their applications across engineering, social science, and biological systems. Relevant for agent-based modeling methodology papers.

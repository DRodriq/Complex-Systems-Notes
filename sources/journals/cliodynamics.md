# Cliodynamics: The Journal of Quantitative History and Cultural Evolution

> The primary journal for cliodynamics — the application of mathematical and computational methods to the study of history and cultural evolution.

## Scope
Cliodynamics publishes quantitative historical research, mathematical models of historical dynamics, and empirical tests of theories about the rise and fall of states, cultural evolution, and social change. The journal was founded by Peter Turchin and is the institutional home for his research program.

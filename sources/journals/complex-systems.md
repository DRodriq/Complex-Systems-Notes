# Complex Systems

> One of the oldest journals in complexity science, founded by Stephen Wolfram in 1987, covering computation, cellular automata, and complex systems theory.

## Scope
Complex Systems was founded alongside Wolfram's early cellular automaton research and publishes work on computation theory, cellular automata, dynamical systems, and formal complexity theory. It has published key early papers in the field.

# SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics

> The Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics journal covering mathematical methods with applications to science and engineering.

## Scope
SIAM JAM publishes rigorous applied mathematical research including dynamical systems, bifurcation theory, mathematical biology, and continuum mechanics. A key venue for the mathematical foundations of complexity science, particularly in dynamics and mathematical biology.

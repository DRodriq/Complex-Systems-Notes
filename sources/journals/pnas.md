# Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)

> The flagship journal of the National Academy of Sciences, publishing high-impact research across all scientific disciplines.

## Scope
PNAS publishes significant findings across biology, physics, social science, and mathematics. Many key complexity science papers appeared here, particularly in network science, evolutionary dynamics, and mathematical biology. The "biological" and "social sciences" sections are most relevant to this vault.

# Complexity

> Wiley journal publishing interdisciplinary research on complex systems, covering emergence, self-organization, networks, and adaptive behavior.

## Scope
Complexity is a broad interdisciplinary journal covering the full range of complex systems research: emergence, self-organization, agent-based modeling, network dynamics, evolutionary computation, and applied complexity. It is associated with SFI and publishes work from across the complexity science community.

# Ecological Modelling

> Elsevier journal publishing mathematical and computational models of ecological systems, including population dynamics, food webs, and spatially explicit ecosystem models.

## Scope
Covers the full range of ecological modeling: individual-based models, population dynamics, ecosystem models, and landscape ecology. A key venue for agent-based and individual-based modeling in ecology, including work following the ODD protocol.

# Theory, Culture & Society

> A SAGE journal at the intersection of social theory, cultural analysis, and critical social science.

## Scope
Theory, Culture & Society publishes social theory, cultural studies, and critical analyses of modernity. Relevant to the vault for philosophical engagements with complexity, systems thinking, and the sociology of science, including discussions of complexity science's cultural implications.

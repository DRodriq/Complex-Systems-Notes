# Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems

> A Springer journal focusing on ordinary and partial differential equations and their applications to dynamical systems theory.

## Scope
Publishes research on ODEs, PDEs, and their qualitative analysis including stability, bifurcations, and long-term behavior. Particularly relevant to the mathematical biology and applied dynamical systems threads.

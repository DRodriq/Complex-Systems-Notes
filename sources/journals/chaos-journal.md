# Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science

> The American Institute of Physics journal dedicated to chaos and nonlinear science, covering dynamical systems, bifurcations, synchronization, and complex networks.

## Scope
Chaos publishes research on nonlinear dynamics, chaos theory, pattern formation, synchronization, and complex networks across physics, biology, chemistry, and engineering. It is the primary venue for technical work on dynamical systems with applications to complexity. Editor-in-chief: [[jurgen-kurths]]. Website: https://pubs.aip.org/aip/cha

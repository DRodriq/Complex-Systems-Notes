# Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation (JASSS)

> An open-access journal dedicated to agent-based and other computational modeling of social systems.

## Scope
JASSS is the primary venue for agent-based social simulation research, covering models of economic, political, and social dynamics. It is open access and publishes both simulation models and methodological discussions, including work following the ODD protocol. Associated with [[nigel-gilbert]] and the social simulation community.

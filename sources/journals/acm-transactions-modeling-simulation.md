# ACM Transactions on Modeling and Computer Simulation

> The ACM's primary journal for research on computational modeling and simulation methodologies, including agent-based and discrete-event simulation.

## Scope
Covers the full range of modeling and simulation methods, with particular relevance to agent-based modeling, Monte Carlo methods, and parallel simulation. Relevant to the computational methodology threads in this vault.

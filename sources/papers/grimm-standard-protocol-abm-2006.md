# A Standard Protocol for Describing Individual-Based and Agent-Based Models — Grimm (2006)

> The paper introducing the ODD (Overview, Design Concepts, Details) protocol for standardized description of agent-based and individual-based models in ecology.

## Key Contribution
Grimm and colleagues proposed the ODD protocol to address the problem of inconsistent and incomplete descriptions of ABMs in scientific literature. The protocol structures model descriptions into three components — Overview, Design concepts, and Details — enabling replication, comparison, and cumulative science with ABMs. It has become the standard in ecological and social simulation modeling.

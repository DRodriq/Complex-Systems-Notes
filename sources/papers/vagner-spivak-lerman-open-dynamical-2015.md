# Algebras of Open Dynamical Systems on the Operad of Wiring Diagrams — Vagner, Spivak & Lerman (2015)

> Theory Appl. Categ. (arXiv:1408.1598). Shows that arbitrary open dynamical systems (not restricted to mass-action reaction networks) can be composed via wiring diagrams: the open-dynamical-systems construction is an algebra over Spivak's wiring-diagrams operad.

## Key Contribution

Generalizes the compositional-modelling approach from the specific case of reaction networks to general open dynamical systems described by vector fields on manifolds with input/output projections. Provides the categorical basis for the AlgebraicDynamics.jl package and for compositional treatments of arbitrary ODE-based systems.

# Punctuated Equilibrium and Criticality in a Simple Model of Evolution — Bak, Sneppen (1993)

> The paper introducing the Bak-Sneppen model, showing that a simple model of coevolving species self-organizes to a critical state producing punctuated equilibrium patterns.

## Key Contribution
Bak and Sneppen demonstrated that a minimal model of coevolving species — where the least fit species mutates and affects its neighbors' fitness — self-organizes to a critical state without tuning any parameters. This produces avalanches of coevolutionary change following power-law distributions, providing a complexity-theoretic explanation for punctuated equilibrium patterns in the fossil record.

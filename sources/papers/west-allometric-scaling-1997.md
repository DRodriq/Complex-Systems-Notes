# A General Model for the Origin of Allometric Scaling Laws in Biology — West, Brown, Enquist (1997)

> The landmark paper deriving the 3/4-power scaling of metabolic rate from first principles by modeling biological resource distribution networks as space-filling fractals.

## Key Contribution
West, Brown, and Enquist showed that the quarter-power scaling laws ubiquitous in biology (metabolic rate scales as mass^(3/4), not mass^(2/3)) can be derived from three assumptions: networks that deliver resources to cells, networks that minimize energy loss, and terminal units (capillaries, cells) that are invariant. This unified hundreds of empirical scaling relationships under a single theoretical framework and launched a research program on scaling in cities and companies.

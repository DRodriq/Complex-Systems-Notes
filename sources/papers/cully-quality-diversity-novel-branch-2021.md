# Quality-Diversity Optimization: A Novel Branch of Stochastic Optimization — Cully (2021)

> A survey and positioning paper establishing quality-diversity optimization as a distinct branch of stochastic optimization theory.

## Key Contribution
Cully positions quality-diversity optimization formally within the landscape of stochastic optimization, distinguishing it from single-objective optimization, multi-objective optimization, and novelty search. QD simultaneously maximizes performance and behavioral diversity, maintaining an archive of elite solutions across a user-defined behavioral space. The paper provides theoretical grounding and surveys the state of the field.

# More is Different — Anderson (1972)

> Philip Anderson's seminal essay arguing that at each level of complexity, genuinely new laws emerge that cannot be reduced to those of the level below — the foundational text for emergence in physics.

## Key Contribution
Anderson's short but enormously influential essay in Science argues against the "constructionist hypothesis" — the idea that knowing the laws of physics automatically gives us knowledge of biology, psychology, or economics. At each level of organization, broken symmetry and emergent phenomena create genuinely new science. This established the intellectual basis for complexity science's claim to be irreducible to physics.

# Discrete Exterior Calculus — Desbrun, Hirani, Leok & Marsden (2005)

> arXiv:math/0508341. Expository / synthesis paper that consolidated and widely disseminated the DEC framework introduced in [[sources/papers/hirani-discrete-exterior-calculus-2003|Hirani's 2003 thesis]]. Builds the discrete differential forms, exterior derivative, Hodge star, and discrete vector fields on simplicial complexes, with attention to use in computational mechanics and discrete geometric integration.

## Key Contribution

A clean expository treatment aimed at applied mathematicians, computational geometers, and engineers, with worked examples. Together with Hirani's thesis it constitutes the canonical reference pair for DEC; nearly all subsequent computational work cites this paper.

# Information Theory for Intelligent Systems — DeDeo (2018)

> DeDeo's pedagogical and theoretical contribution applying information theory to the analysis of intelligent systems, social dynamics, and causal reasoning.

## Key Contribution
DeDeo develops an information-theoretic framework suitable for analyzing intelligent and social systems, going beyond Shannon's original communications context. The paper addresses how information theory can be applied to causal inference, model comparison, and the quantification of social information processing, making it a practical guide for complexity researchers working with data from intelligent systems.

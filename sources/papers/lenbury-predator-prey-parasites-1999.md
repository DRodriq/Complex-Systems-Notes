# Predator-Prey Interaction Coupled by Parasitic Infection: Limit Cycles and Chaotic Behavior — Lenbury, Rattanamongkonkul (1999)

> A mathematical analysis of predator-prey systems modified by parasitic infection, demonstrating conditions for limit cycles and chaotic dynamics in three-species host-parasite systems.

## Key Contribution
Lenbury and Rattanamongkonkul analyze how parasitic infection of prey modifies predator-prey dynamics, finding that the added dimension of parasite dynamics can produce rich behavior including sustained oscillations and chaotic attractors. This contributes to understanding how parasitism complicates ecological community dynamics.

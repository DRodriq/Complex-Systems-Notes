# Selective Predation and Productivity Jointly Drive Complex Behavior in Host-Parasite Systems — Hall, Duffy, Caceres (2005)

> A paper demonstrating that selective predation on infected hosts, combined with resource productivity, generates complex dynamical behaviors in host-parasite-predator systems.

## Key Contribution
Hall, Duffy, and Caceres show in a Daphnia-fungal parasite system that when predators selectively consume infected individuals, the interaction between predation, productivity, and parasitism creates complex dynamics including cycles and potentially chaos. This illustrates how ecological complexity emerges from tri-trophic interactions.

# The Operad of Wiring Diagrams — Spivak (2013)

> arXiv:1305.0297. Establishes that wiring diagrams — boxes-with-ports connected by wires — form an **operad**, and that assigning a concrete system to each box and the composite system to each diagram is an **algebra** over that operad.

## Key Contribution

The operadic framing formalizes the engineer's intuition that complex systems are built by drawing boxes and connecting them with wires. The flexibility of "what counts as a system" is huge — dynamical systems, databases, processes, relations all support such algebras — which makes the construction recur throughout applied category theory. Foundational for the subsequent line of work on open dynamical systems composed via wiring diagrams (Vagner–Spivak–Lerman 2015) and the AlgebraicJulia software stack.

# Neuroevolution of Augmenting Topologies — Stanley, Miikkulainen (2002)

> The paper introducing NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies), an algorithm that evolves both the weights and structure of neural networks through genetic algorithms.

## Key Contribution
Stanley and Miikkulainen introduced NEAT, which solves two fundamental problems in neuroevolution: how to evolve neural network structure (topology) alongside weights, and how to protect structural innovations from being eliminated before they have time to optimize. NEAT uses historical markings to align genomes for crossover and speciation to protect innovation. It became foundational in evolutionary robotics and was later extended to HyperNEAT.

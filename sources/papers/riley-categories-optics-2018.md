# Categories of Optics — Riley (2018)

> arXiv:1809.00738. Unifies many variants of lenses — monomorphic and polymorphic lenses, prisms, traversals, grates — under the general **optic** construction, defined via coends.

## Key Contribution

The optic construction takes an indexed family of categories and produces a category whose morphisms are bidirectional `(get, put)`-style processes generalizing classical lenses. Different choices of the underlying indexed family recover different optic variants. This unification was a major clarification in the lens literature and underwrote subsequent applied work in categorical cybernetics and compositional game theory.

# Scaling in Biology — West, Brown (2001)

> West and Brown's synthesis paper reviewing the evidence for and theoretical understanding of scaling laws in biology, from metabolism to lifespan to population growth rates.

## Key Contribution
West and Brown review the empirical evidence for quarter-power scaling laws across biology — metabolic rates, lifespans, heart rates, genome lengths, and population densities all scale with body mass following simple power laws with quarter-power exponents. They argue these laws arise from the fractal geometry of biological distribution networks and constitute a fundamental theoretical framework for biology.

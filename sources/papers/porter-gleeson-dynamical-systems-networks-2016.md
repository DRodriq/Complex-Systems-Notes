# Dynamical Systems on Networks: A Tutorial (Porter & Gleeson, 2016)

Frontiers in Applied Dynamical Systems: Reviews and Tutorials 4. Free at
arXiv:1403.7663.

Compact tutorial covering synchronization (Kuramoto), epidemic spreading
(SIS/SIR on networks), opinion dynamics, and other dynamical processes on
fixed network topologies. Aimed at readers with dynamical-systems background
who want to extend to network substrates.

## Why it's here

Bridge document between Strogatz-style dynamical systems and network science.
Natural early-entry paper on the network-science track — leverages existing
dynamical-systems intuition to install the "dynamics on networks" frame.

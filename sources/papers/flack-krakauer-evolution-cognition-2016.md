# What Evolution Means for Cognition — Flack, Krakauer (2016)

> A paper by Flack and Krakauer arguing that evolution and cognition are fundamentally information-processing phenomena, and that an information-theoretic perspective illuminates both.

## Key Contribution
Flack and Krakauer develop the argument that cognition — broadly construed as adaptive information processing — is a product of evolutionary processes and can be understood through the same computational and information-theoretic framework. They propose that coarse-graining and abstraction are central to both evolution and cognition.

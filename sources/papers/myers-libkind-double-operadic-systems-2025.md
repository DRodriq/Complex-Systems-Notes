# Towards a Double Operadic Theory of Systems — Myers & Libkind (2025)

> arXiv:2505.18329. A unified framework for categorical systems theory that packages open systems, their interactions, and the maps between them into a single structure: a symmetric monoidal loose right module of systems over a symmetric monoidal double category.

## Key Contribution

This is the consolidation step the categorical-systems-theory program had been building toward. Earlier work offered several distinct categorical treatments of open systems (structured cospans, the operad of wiring diagrams, Poly-based interactive systems); this paper provides a common double-operadic home in which systems, their composition (interactions), and the structure-respecting maps between them all live coherently. The "loose right module over a double category" packaging is what lets results from the different communities be stated and combined uniformly.

For a builder, the significance is a single, principled substrate on which world-subsystems and agents are the *same kind of object*, composed by the same machinery — the formal underpinning for a clean, modular, handoff-able compositional modeling laboratory.

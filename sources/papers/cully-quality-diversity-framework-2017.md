# Quality and Diversity Optimization: A Unifying Modular Framework — Cully (2017)

> A paper providing a unified framework for quality-diversity (QD) algorithms, showing how MAP-Elites and related approaches share common structure and can be analyzed together.

## Key Contribution
Cully provides a modular, unified view of quality-diversity optimization algorithms, showing that MAP-Elites, CVT-MAP-Elites, and related approaches share a common computational structure: maintain an archive of diverse, high-performing solutions across a behavioral descriptor space. This framework enables systematic comparison and combination of QD components.

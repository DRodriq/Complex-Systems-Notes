# Safeguarded AI: Constructing Guaranteed Safety — Dalrymple (2024)

> ARIA programme thesis (Advanced Research + Invention Agency, UK), 2024. The funding-scale vision for provably safe AI: pair a **formal world-model** with **verified agents** whose policies come with mathematical safety guarantees, rather than relying on empirical red-teaming alone.

## Key Contribution

Dalrymple ("davidad") sets out a research programme in which an AI system's safety is *constructed* — a quantitative world-model expresses what can happen, a specification states what must not, and an agent is accepted only with a machine-checkable proof that it meets the spec against the model. The categorical relevance is structural: the programme's TA1.1 theory track adopts double categorical systems theory (Myers–Libkind) as the mathematical substrate for modelling, specification, and verification — the funded **"DCST for Safeguarded AI"** project. This is the institutional reason the categorical-systems-theory line has live, well-resourced applied stakes, and the application context both the vault's CT cluster and the SimLab design docs repeatedly invoke.

# Hopf Bifurcation Analysis for a Predator-Prey System of Holling and Leslie Type — Hsu, Hwang (1999)

> A mathematical analysis of Hopf bifurcation conditions and limit cycle existence in a predator-prey system combining Holling functional response and Leslie-Gower dynamics.

## Key Contribution
Hsu and Hwang provide rigorous mathematical analysis of when Hopf bifurcations occur in the Holling-Leslie predator-prey system, establishing conditions for the existence and stability of limit cycles. This contributes to the mathematical theory of ecological oscillations.

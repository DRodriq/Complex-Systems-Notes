# Double Categories of Open Dynamical Systems — Myers (2021)

> arXiv:2005.05956 (EPTCS 333, Applied Category Theory 2020). The research paper behind Myers' *Categorical Systems Theory* book draft: it organises open dynamical systems and the maps between them into a **double category**, separating "systems and their behaviours" (one direction) from "system maps that respect interfaces" (the other).

## Key Contribution

This is the formal seed of the whole categorical-systems-theory programme. Myers shows that open dynamical systems compose along their interfaces (the operadic/wiring-diagram direction) *and* admit structure-preserving maps between them, and that these two kinds of arrow fit together as a double category. That two-dimensional packaging is what later lets the book draft, and then [[sources/papers/myers-libkind-double-operadic-systems-2025|Myers–Libkind 2025]], state and combine results across the previously separate communities (cospans, wiring diagrams, Poly). The vault held the book draft and the 2025 consolidation but not this originating paper — it is the load-bearing primary source under the [[concepts/category-theory/categorical-systems-theory]] node.

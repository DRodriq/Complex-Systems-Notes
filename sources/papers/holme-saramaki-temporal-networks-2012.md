# Temporal networks (Holme & Saramäki, 2012)

Physics Reports 519:3, 97–125 (2012). Free at arXiv:1108.1780.

The field-defining review of temporal networks: graphs in which edges carry
timestamps and dynamics depend on timing, not just topology. Covers
representations (contact sequences, interval graphs), temporal distances and
paths, burstiness, temporal motifs, and how epidemic thresholds and
synchronization differ from static-network predictions.

## Why it's here

Primary entry point for temporal-network thinking, directly relevant to Dane's
IE streaming work (cluster genealogy DAGs are a temporal network; IE
propagation is a temporal network). The shift from static to temporal is one
of the dimensional expansions Dane is explicitly seeking from this track.

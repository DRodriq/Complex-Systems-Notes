# Scale and Growth in Cities and Companies — West (2009)

> West's paper extending allometric scaling laws from biology to cities and companies, showing that cities and companies obey distinct scaling relationships with city size.

## Key Contribution
West and colleagues demonstrated that urban quantities scale superlinearly with city population (wages, patents, GDP scale as population^1.15) while infrastructure scales sublinearly (roads, gas stations scale as population^0.85). Companies, by contrast, scale sublinearly like biological organisms. This revealed universal scaling principles governing human agglomerations and laid groundwork for [[west-scale-2017]].

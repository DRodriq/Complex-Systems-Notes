# Neural Networks are a New Type of Dynamical System — Shlizerman (2023)

> Shlizerman's argument that modern trained neural networks constitute a new category of dynamical system with distinctive properties not captured by classical dynamical systems theory.

## Key Contribution
Shlizerman argues that trained neural networks — particularly recurrent networks — represent a new kind of dynamical system that differs from classical continuous dynamical systems in important ways: they are learned, their structure encodes information, and their dynamics are shaped by training data. This positions neural networks as objects of study for dynamical systems theory while recognizing their novelty.

# A Compositional Framework for Reaction Networks — Baez & Pollard (2017)

> Rev. Math. Phys. (arXiv:1704.02051). Defines *open* reaction networks and proves that the assignment "open reaction network → rate equation (vector field)" is a **functor** — the rate equation of the composite genuinely is glued from the parts' rate equations.

## Key Contribution

A keystone "the rule composes" result. The functorial semantics shows that for the compositional modelling of reaction networks, the *local rule* is compositional and scales correctly under composition. (The *qualitative behaviour* — attractors, limit cycles, bifurcations — is a separate question, and is precisely where [[concepts/category-theory/generative-effects|generative effects]] enter.)

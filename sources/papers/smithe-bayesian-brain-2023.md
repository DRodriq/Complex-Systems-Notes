# Mathematical Foundations for a Compositional Account of the Bayesian Brain — Smithe (2023)

> DPhil thesis. Develops a categorical account of **active inference / the Bayesian brain**, treating agents-with-internal-models as compositional structures within the categorical-cybernetics line.

## Key Contribution

Brings active-inference and predictive-coding accounts of cognition into the categorical-cybernetics framework: agents with generative models whose perception and action arise from minimizing free energy are recast as morphisms in suitable categories, composable along agent/environment interfaces. Provides the most developed categorical treatment to date of the cognitive-science side of the cybernetics-renaissance line of work.

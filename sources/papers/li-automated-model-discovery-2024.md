# Automated Statistical Model Discovery with Language Models — Li, Fox, Goodman (2024)

> A paper exploring the use of large language models to automatically discover statistical models for data, combining LLM generation with probabilistic program synthesis.

## Key Contribution
Li, Fox, and Goodman demonstrate that language models can be used to propose and evaluate statistical models for empirical data, potentially automating parts of the scientific modeling process. This is a contribution to the emerging field of AI-assisted scientific discovery and connects to probabilistic programming and Bayesian inference.

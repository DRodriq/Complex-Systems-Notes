# Structured Cospans — Baez & Courser (2020)

> Theory Appl. Categ. (arXiv:1911.04630). Generalizes Fong's decorated-cospan construction to **structured cospans**, providing a cleaner categorical setting for open systems with boundaries.

## Key Contribution

A structured cospan is a cospan in a category $\mathbf{A}$ whose feet are in the image of a functor $L : \mathbf{X} \to \mathbf{A}$ — typically with $\mathbf{X}$ much simpler than $\mathbf{A}$. This generalizes decorated cospans while making the categorical properties (double-categorical structure, symmetric monoidal structure) cleaner. The construction underlies the open-Petri-net, open-reaction-network, and open-graph machinery used throughout subsequent compositional-modelling work.

# Explaining the Past with ABM: On Modelling Philosophy — Lake (2013)

> A philosophical examination of what agent-based models explain and how they should be evaluated as scientific tools, particularly in archaeological applications.

## Key Contribution
Lake addresses the epistemological status of ABMs in archaeology and social science, asking whether simulation models explain or merely redescribe phenomena. He argues that ABMs provide a specific kind of explanation — showing how observed patterns could be generated by specific mechanisms — and discusses how this "how-possibly" explanation differs from traditional scientific explanation.

# Universal Scaling of Production and Innovation in Cities — West (2012)

> West's paper establishing universal superlinear scaling laws for innovation and productive output in cities worldwide.

## Key Contribution
West and collaborators demonstrate that measures of urban innovation and socioeconomic output (patents, R&D expenditure, wages, GDP) scale superlinearly with city population across countries and time periods, with a universal exponent near 1.15. This suggests that cities function as social reactors where human interaction rates drive innovation in a predictable, scale-invariant way.

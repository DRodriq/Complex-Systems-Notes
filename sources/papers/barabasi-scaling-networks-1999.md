# Emergence of Scaling in Random Networks — Barabasi (1999)

> The landmark paper by Barabasi and Albert introducing the preferential attachment model to explain the emergence of scale-free (power-law degree distribution) networks.

## Key Contribution
Barabasi and Albert showed that networks growing by adding nodes that preferentially connect to already well-connected nodes naturally develop power-law degree distributions — producing "scale-free" networks with hubs. This "Barabasi-Albert model" explained why the World Wide Web, citation networks, and many biological networks exhibit scale-free topology, and launched the modern era of network science.

# Nondeterministic Behaviours in Double Categorical Systems Theory — Myers et al. (2025)

> arXiv:2502.02517. Extends double-categorical systems theory (DCST) to handle nondeterministic behaviours, sharpening the "behaviour" half of the systems/behaviours split that the double-categorical framework is built around.

## Key Contribution

DCST organizes open systems so that one direction of the double category carries *systems and their behaviours* and the other carries *interface-respecting maps*. This paper develops the behaviour side for the nondeterministic case — important because most genuinely interesting complex/agentic systems are not deterministic, and a compositional account of their behaviour is needed for the framework to apply beyond toy deterministic dynamics.

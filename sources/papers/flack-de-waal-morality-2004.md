# Any Animal Whatever: Darwinian Building Blocks of Morality — Flack, de Waal (2004)

> A paper arguing that the building blocks of human morality — empathy, reciprocity, conflict resolution — are present in non-human primates and arose through natural selection.

## Key Contribution
Flack and de Waal synthesize behavioral evidence from non-human primates to argue that moral sentiments are not uniquely human but are Darwinian adaptations with evolutionary precursors in other social species. This challenges views of morality as purely cultural or uniquely human and connects moral behavior to complexity science through the building-blocks concept.

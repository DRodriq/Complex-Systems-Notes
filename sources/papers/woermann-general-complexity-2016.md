# General Complexity: A Philosophical and Critical Perspective — Woermann, Human, Preiser (2016)

> A philosophical paper examining "general complexity" as a theoretical framework drawing on Edgar Morin's work, proposing a critical and reflexive approach to complexity thinking.

## Key Contribution
The authors develop the concept of "general complexity" following Morin, arguing for a reflexive, self-critical approach to complexity science that acknowledges the observer's role. They distinguish general complexity from "restricted complexity" (formal, mathematical approaches) and argue for an epistemology adequate to genuinely complex phenomena.

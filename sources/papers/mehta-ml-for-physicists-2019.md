# A high-bias, low-variance introduction to Machine Learning for physicists (Mehta et al., 2019)

Physics Reports 810, 1–124 (2019). Free at arXiv:1803.08823.

Comprehensive review framing modern machine learning through the lens of
statistical mechanics. Covers ML foundations, supervised/unsupervised methods,
neural networks, deep learning, energy-based models, generative models — all
with partition functions, free energies, variational principles, and RG ideas
made explicit. Includes Jupyter-notebook exercises.

## Why it's here

Bridge paper pairing directly with Sethna in the pathway. Translates the
stat-mech ground truth into ML language — variational inference as free-energy
minimization, RG as representation learning, phase transitions in learning
dynamics. Perfect fit for Dane's workflow: stat mech plus code plus NN-from-scratch
experience compose here.

# Compositional Game Theory — Ghani, Hedges, Winschel & Zahn (2018)

> LICS 2018 (arXiv:1603.04641). Founding paper of **compositional game theory**: each game is recast as an **open game** — a morphism with input/output interfaces, built from lens-like structures — that composes with other open games to build larger games.

## Key Contribution

The paper recovers Nash equilibria as fixed-point conditions on composite open games, and gives a graphical notation for plays and counter-plays. The lens-based formulation explains the bidirectional flow of plays (forward) and payoffs/best-responses (backward), turning a notoriously monolithic theory into a compositional one.

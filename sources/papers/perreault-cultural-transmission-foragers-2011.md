# Mobility-Driven Cultural Transmission along the Forager-Collector Continuum — Perreault, Brantingham (2011)

> A paper modeling how mobility patterns among hunter-gatherers shape cultural transmission rates and the maintenance of cultural diversity.

## Key Contribution
Perreault and Brantingham model how variation in mobility along the forager-collector continuum affects the rate of cultural transmission between groups and the resulting patterns of cultural diversity and convergence. Higher mobility foragers have more cultural contact, while collectors maintain more isolated traditions. This connects cultural evolution models to subsistence ecology.

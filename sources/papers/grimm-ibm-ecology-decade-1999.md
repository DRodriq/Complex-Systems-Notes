# Ten Years of Individual-Based Modelling in Ecology: What Have We Learned and What Could We Learn in the Future — Grimm (1999)

> A review paper assessing a decade of individual-based and agent-based modeling in ecology, identifying lessons learned and future directions.

## Key Contribution
Grimm surveys ten years of individual-based modeling (IBM) in ecology and finds both methodological progress and persistent challenges. Key lessons include the importance of adaptive behavior, the difficulty of model validation, and the need for standardized model description. The paper set the agenda for the next decade of ecological ABM and eventually led to the ODD protocol.

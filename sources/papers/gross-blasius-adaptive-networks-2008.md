# Adaptive coevolutionary networks: a review (Gross & Blasius, 2008)

Journal of the Royal Society Interface 5(20), 259–271. Free at arXiv:0709.1858.

Defines and reviews adaptive networks: systems in which the network topology
coevolves with the state of the nodes, producing feedback between structure
and dynamics. Covers epidemic, opinion, neural, and ecological examples and
the phenomena (bistability, oscillations, phase transitions) that emerge only
in adaptive settings.

## Why it's here

Defines the "dynamics of networks" frame (topology evolves) as distinct from
"dynamics on networks" (topology fixed). Coevolving structure and dynamics is
the form of network thinking most relevant to IE work, where cluster and
actor-network structure changes as content propagates.

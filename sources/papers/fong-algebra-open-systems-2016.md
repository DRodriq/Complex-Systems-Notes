# The Algebra of Open and Interconnected Systems — Fong (2016)

> Fong's DPhil thesis (Oxford, 2016). Introduces **decorated cospans** as a categorical formalism for systems with explicit boundaries, and gives [[generative-effects]] their formal home.

## Key Contribution

A decorated cospan equips an ordinary cospan $X \to S \leftarrow Y$ with extra structure (the "decoration") on the apex object $S$. This lets you describe many flavours of open system — circuits, Petri nets, signal-flow graphs, Markov processes — within one categorical setting, and to compose them by pushout. Provides the technical apparatus that *Seven Sketches* later popularized.

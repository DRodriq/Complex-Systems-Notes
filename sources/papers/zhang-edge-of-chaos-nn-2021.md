# Edge of Chaos as a Guiding Principle for Modern Neural Network Training — Lin Zhang, Ling Feng, Kan Chen, Choy Heng Lai (2021)

> A paper arguing that the "edge of chaos" principle from complexity science applies to modern deep neural networks, where optimal training occurs near criticality.

## Key Contribution
Zhang et al. apply the edge-of-chaos framework to deep neural networks, showing empirically and theoretically that neural networks achieve optimal learning dynamics when initialized and trained near the critical boundary between ordered and chaotic propagation regimes. This connects complexity science concepts to practical deep learning methodology.

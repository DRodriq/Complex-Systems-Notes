# Complex Dynamics in Biological Systems Arising from Multiple Limit Cycle Bifurcation — Yu, Lin (2016)

> A technical paper analyzing the emergence of complex dynamics in biological ODE systems through multiple simultaneous limit cycle bifurcations.

## Key Contribution
Yu and Lin analyze conditions under which biological dynamical systems can generate multiple nested limit cycles through higher-order Hopf bifurcations, producing complex oscillatory behavior. The work contributes to understanding multi-rhythm biological phenomena such as neural oscillations and cell cycle dynamics.

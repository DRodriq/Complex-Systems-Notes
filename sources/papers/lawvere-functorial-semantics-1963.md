# Functorial Semantics of Algebraic Theories — Lawvere (1963)

> Lawvere's PhD thesis (Columbia, 1963). Establishes the stance that an algebraic *theory* is a category and a *model* of that theory is a structure-preserving functor — the founding move under most subsequent work in applied category theory.

## Key Contribution

The reformulation: a theory is a category $T$ whose objects are types and whose morphisms are operations of the theory; a model is a functor $T \to \mathbf{Set}$ (or into another suitable target). Composition of operations and composition of model maps are then both categorical composition. This dissolved a long-standing tension between syntax and semantics by making both sides categorical objects and the interpretation a morphism between them.

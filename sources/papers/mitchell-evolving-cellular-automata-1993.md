# Evolving Cellular Automata to Perform Computation — Mitchell, Hraber, Crutchfield (1993)

> A paper using genetic algorithms to evolve cellular automata rules that perform computational tasks, exploring how computation emerges in distributed systems.

## Key Contribution
Mitchell, Hraber, and Crutchfield evolved cellular automata rules using genetic algorithms to perform the density classification task (determining whether a majority of cells are 0 or 1 from initial conditions). The work revealed how difficult it is to evolve globally coordinated computation from local interactions, and illuminated the structure of computational strategies that do emerge — including "particles" and their interactions.

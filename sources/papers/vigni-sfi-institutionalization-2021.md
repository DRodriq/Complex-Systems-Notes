# The Failed Institutionalization of "Complexity Science": A Focus on the Santa Fe Institute's Legitimization Strategy — Vigni (2021)

> A sociological analysis of the Santa Fe Institute's struggle to establish "complexity science" as a recognized academic discipline and the limits of its legitimization strategy.

## Key Contribution
Vigni examines, from a sociology of science perspective, why complexity science as promoted by SFI did not become an established discipline with its own departments and degrees, despite intellectual success. He analyzes SFI's legitimization strategies, boundary work, and the tensions between disciplinary and interdisciplinary identity that constrained institutionalization.

## Vault Relevance
Provides critical sociological perspective on the [[Santa Fe Institute]] and the institutional history of complexity science; relevant to the history and philosophy of the field.

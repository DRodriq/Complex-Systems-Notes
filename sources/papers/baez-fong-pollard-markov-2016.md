# A Compositional Framework for Markov Processes — Baez, Fong & Pollard (2016)

> J. Math. Phys. (arXiv:1508.06448). Treats open Markov processes as morphisms in a category and shows that "black-boxing" — the assignment of an open Markov process to its steady-state input/output relation — is a **functor**.

## Key Contribution

A second canonical example of functorial semantics in compositional dynamical-systems modelling: the input/output behaviour of an open stochastic system in steady state composes the same way the system does. Like the Baez–Pollard rate-equation result, it formalizes a sense in which "the rule (input/output relation) composes," even though more detailed dynamical descriptions may not.

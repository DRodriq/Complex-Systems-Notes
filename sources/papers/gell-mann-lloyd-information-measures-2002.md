# Information Measures, Effective Complexity, and Total Information — Gell-Mann, Lloyd (2002)

> A paper formalizing Gell-Mann's concept of effective complexity and relating it to total information and algorithmic information content.

## Key Contribution
Gell-Mann and Lloyd formalize the distinction between algorithmic information content (description length, closely related to Shannon entropy for random systems) and effective complexity (the length of descriptions of regularities). They introduce "total information" as the sum of effective complexity and randomness, providing a richer taxonomy of information in complex systems than Shannon entropy alone.
